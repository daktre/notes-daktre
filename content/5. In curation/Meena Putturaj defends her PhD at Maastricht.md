# Meena's PhD defense

*Note from Dec 18, 2023 about my 2nd PhD student who dfended her PhD at Maastrict University supported by IPH Bengaluru*

Tomorrow, Meena Putturaj, one of my 1st PhD students will defend her PhD at Maastricht University (@CAPHRI_UM) on patient (health) rights in Karnataka; her PhD is part of core health policy & systems research work @iphindia; 

A thread on her prolific outputs from her PhD (1/n):

Our earliest collaboration began when we both sought to examine the need to increase autonomy & participation of nurses in health policy & systems; something that continues to be doctor-dominated; we wrote a commentary on this in @IndJMedEthics (2/n)

🔗: https://ijme.in/articles/enhancing-the-autonomy-of-indian-nurses/

![[x-1736701463261589817-1.jpg]]

Meena trained as a community health nurse & did her MPH at @ITMantwerp; for her masters dissertation she was already interested in health governance; she studied private health sector regulation in Karnataka in the context of KPMEA;👇🏽(3/n)

https://www.nmji.in/content/141/2021/34/2/Images/NatlMedJIndia_2021_34_2_100_326754.pdf

![[x-1736701474187665538-1.jpg]]

Continuing her interest in health governance, she wrote up a very interesting PhD proposal to study patient (health) rights in healthcare facilities in Karnataka, for which she was awarded the highly competitive DGD scholarship at @ITMantwerp; (4/n)
https://iphindia.org/dr-meena-putturaj-phd-scholar/

Her PhD began with systematic (scoping) review: 

“how do the multilevel actors, institutional structures, processes interact & influence patient rights implementation in healthcare facilities? How do they work at what level & in which contexts?” (5/n)

🔗 https://bmjopen.bmj.com/content/10/10/e038927.abstract

![[x-1736701490490950123-1.jpg]]

Based on this, she produced “an analytical framework on grievance redressal for patient rights violations in health facilities”; she “used lawsuits on patient rights violations from Indian Supreme Court to analyse relevance to Indian context” (6/n)

🔗: https://academic.oup.com/heapol/article-abstract/36/9/1470/6300626

![[x-1736701500284633249-1.jpg]]

Some of the key results from her work were already pointing towards health governance aspects that she would deepen in her upcoming empirical case studies (7/n)

Read her key results here from same paper above in @HPP_LSHTM 👇🏽

![[x-1736701508698488913-1.jpg]]

Next, she examined a particularly critical case of patient rights violations to “explore the power dynamics involved in resolving grievances raised by healthcare-seeking individuals” leading to this landmark paper in @GlobalHealthBMJ (8/n)

🔗: https://gh.bmj.com/content/7/5/e008626.abstract

![[x-1736701516906725589-1.jpg]]

Among other things, she explored the governmentally of grievance redressal 👉🏽 “ The State bureaucracy enacted its power by creating a maze of organisational units and by fragmenting the grievance redressal across various
bureaucratic units”; (9/n)

![[x-1736701524867465515-1.jpg]]

![[x-1736701524867465515-2.jpg]]

She then sought to examine how health workers understand & practice patient rights in everyday context in 10 health facilities in Karnataka (& how it is understood in state policy documents) & the title of resulting article is rather telling 👇🏽(10/n)

🔗 https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s12939-023-01850-5

![[x-1736701535126728774-1.jpg]]

With these above pieces of scholarship as the basis, she has produced a robust dissertation on realising patient rights in our settings which led to:👇🏽(11/n)

Her thesis propositions @maasuniversity 🔗: https://cris.maastrichtuniversity.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/161639419/p8082.pdf

![[x-1736701544677191851-1.jpg]]

What’s next for her? We @iphindia were so delighted to hear that she’s been awarded a DBT/Wellcome Trust @India_Alliance Early Career Fellowship in public health to study health governance among Adivasi collectives as her work moves ahead in patient rights (12/n)

![[x-1736701559147491331-1.jpg]]

And for us @iphindia, yet another joyful occasion as we celebrate one of our colleagues’ achievements in hope that she/we continue “paying it forward”; this is as much a milestone for me as her! (13/n)

https://www.itg.be/en/health-stories/articles/paying-it-forward

If you survived this thread till here, please do watch her dissertation live to be streamed at link below at 1430 Indian time on 19 December 2023 (n/n)

🔗: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HU5C2pCOdx8
