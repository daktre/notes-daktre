Got inspired by a rain tree that was leaning into the lake for this poem about the “exotics” (plants that are not “native” and hence looked down upon generally speaking among ecologists).

After this run: https://strava.app.link/vOtQwq3W05b

# The rain tree

It reaches as if  
defying the physics  
of being a tree

with one trunk  
Reaching out as much  
to the skies  
as to the floor

providing shade and  
a welcome  
yet an exotic  
Not from the place  
brought in by another  
not meant to belong

But yet... How beautifully  
The rain tree has  
claimed belonged and merged  
into our lakes Pedestrian walks And  
our poetry and lore

Even if it be that  
among Plants and wildlife  
The exotic shall remain at bay

May our lives be enriched  
by another even from far away

Recalls my earlier poem from another rain tree another year at the Rain tree cafe in Bengaluru

Under a Rain Tree

I reach, I hold  
I care, I wither;

I shed, I heal,  
I break but recover;

I watch, I wear,  
I brave & I figure…

I will live on,  
one way or another…
