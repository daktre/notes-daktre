To be, or not to be
is not a choice,
for the one who is

So, here’s raising a toast
for your being.

You are
and that makes the world
mildly massy,
moderately grassy and
distinctly gassy.

On this day,
here’s wishing
for your continued being,

For, none are as blessed,
As those that are blessed for,
by near-dears and,
the non sequitur. 

Birthday wishes!

Wish I could say
“Have a blast…”
like some greeting card from corner store...

but these be troubled times
even for lame Archies’ lines!
