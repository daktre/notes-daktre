# Sorrow

From where it comes
A deep marsh
An unknown wilderness
Somewhere in the spaces
Where the mind does not see
Nor the sound heard
But the heart feels
From where it comes

Nobody knows, but they feel
Unseen & unheard it fills
the heart, the mind & the sight
With a flood of a sensation
Unheld, Unbound, Untamed

From where it comes?
The heart that feels
The sight that sees
The mind that thinks
Altogether one in sorrow

One day it heals...?
