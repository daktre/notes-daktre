# Video Resource: Patterns, process and action on tribal health

**Event**: Sangath Research Webinar / Monthly Research Webinar
**Date**: May 2022
**Role**: Presenter

## Context
This technical research webinar dives deep into Dr. Srinivas's work with the **Theta Project** (Mapping of process and outcomes towards health equity) and the **Centre for Training, Research and Innovation in Tribal Health (CTRITH)**.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways

### 1. Unique Health Challenges of Tribal Populations
*   **Argument**: Tribal communities face a "double burden" of disease and significant barriers to access that are distinct from other rural populations.
*   **Takeaway**: Generic "rural health" policies often fail in tribal areas; specific "tribal health" strategies are needed.

### 2. Transformative Action
*   **Argument**: Research shouldn't just document problems; it should lead to "transformative action."
*   **Takeaway**: The goal of the CTRITH project is to innovate new models of care that are culturally appropriate and effective.
