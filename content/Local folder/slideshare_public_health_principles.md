## Context
Educational slides defining the core **principles of public health**.

## Core Concepts
*   **Population Focus**: Public health treats the community, not just the individual.
*   **Prevention**: Focus on upstream determinants rather than downstream cure.
*   **Equity**: Health is a human right.
