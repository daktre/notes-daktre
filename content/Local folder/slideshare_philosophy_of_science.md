# Presentation: What’s in the method? Philosophy of science in public health

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/whats-in-the-method-brief-introduction-to-philosophy-of-science-in-public-health-research/250904376)

## Context
A lecture/presentation exploring the **Philosophy of Science** underlying public health research methods. It challenges researchers to examine their own worldviews (ontology) and how they construct knowledge (epistemology).

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways
*   **Methods are Political**: Research methods are not neutral tools; they carry the bias and worldview of the researcher.
*   **Reflexivity**: Public health researchers must be "reflexive"—constantly questioning how their own position influences their findings.
*   **Social Constructs**: Health itself is a social construct, not just a biological reality.
