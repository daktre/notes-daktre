## Context
A theoretical examination of what constitutes legitimate "knowledge" in health systems research (HPSR).

## Core Arguments
*   **Epistemic Justice**: recognizing community lived experience as valid evidence alongside statistical data.
*   **Participation**: Moving participation from "consultation" to "control".
