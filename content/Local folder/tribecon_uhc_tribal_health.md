# Video Resource: Tribal Health & Universal Health Coverage

**Event**: TRIBECON - National Conference on Tribal Health
**Date**: 2019
**Role**: Plenary Speaker

## Context
A plenary talk addressing the intersection of **Universal Health Coverage (UHC)** and **Tribal Health**. The central question is whether UHC models, which are often designed for general populations, effectively serve tribal communities or if they inevitably leave them behind.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways

### 1. The "Last Mile" Problem
*   **Argument**: UHC often fails in tribal areas not just because of a lack of resources, but because the *model* of delivery is mismatched with the geographical and cultural reality of tribal life.
*   **Takeaway**: Treating tribal health as merely a "last mile" delivery problem ignores the specific structural barriers they face.

### 2. Equity vs. Equality
*   **Argument**: Providing "equal" services (same clinics, same doctors) does not result in equitable health outcomes when the baseline disadvantage is so high.
*   **Takeaway**: Affirmative, targeted interventions are needed.
