# Presentation: Global Health & Health Systems: Health Systems as Social Systems

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/global-health-health-systems-health-systems-as-social-systems/68846372)

## Context
Framing health systems not as mechanical "delivery pipelines" but as complex **social systems** embedded in political and economic contexts.

## Core Arguments
*   **Complexity**: Linear interventions often fail in complex social systems.
*   **People-Centred**: Systems consist of people (providers, patients), not just infrastructure.
