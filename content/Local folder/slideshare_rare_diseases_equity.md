# Presentation: Equity in representation of rare diseases in India

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/equity-in-representation-of-rare-diseases-in-india/251572423)

## Context
A critical analysis of how "rare diseases" are prioritized within the Indian health system, contrasting public health utilitarianism/efficiency with individual rights and equity.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways
*   **The Equity-Efficiency Trade-off**: Public health often prioritizes conditions that affect the "many" (efficiency). Rare diseases, affecting the "few," test the limits of this logic.
*   **Socio-economic Disparities**: Access to care for rare diseases is often determined by ability to pay, creating deep inequities.
*   **Justice Framework**: The presentation argues for a justice-based framework where "rarity" does not justify neglect.
