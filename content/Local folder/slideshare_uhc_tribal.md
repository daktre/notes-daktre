# Presentation: Universal Health Coverage & Tribal Health

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/universal-health-coverage-tribal-health/68832617)

## Context
Presentation on the specific challenges of implementing **Universal Health Coverage (UHC)** in tribal contexts.

## Core Arguments
*   **Exclusion**: Standard UHC packages often exclude services vital to tribal populations (e.g., sickle cell screening, nutrition).
*   **Access**: Financial protection (insurance) doesn't help if there are no providers in the forest.
