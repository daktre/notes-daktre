## Context
Detailed overview of the **THETA Project** (Towards Health Equity & Transformative Action), a major research initiative focused on tribal health outcomes.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways
*   **Mapping Inequities**: The project maps not just disease prevalence but the "process" of exclusion that leads to poor health.
*   **Structural Barriers**: Highlights specific structural barriers like distance, discrimination, and lack of culturally appropriate care.
*   **Action Oriented**: The goal is not just study but "transformative action"—changing how the system delivers care.
