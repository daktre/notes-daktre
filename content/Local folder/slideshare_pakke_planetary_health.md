# Presentation: Planetary Health Information Center at Pakke Tiger Reserve

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/planetary-health-information-center-at-pakke-tiger-reserve/250896640)

## Context
A case study on the co-production of a **Planetary Health Information Center** at the Pakke Tiger Reserve. This is a practical example of "One Health" in action.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways
*   **Co-Production**: The center wasn't built *for* the community but *with* them (forest staff, locals).
*   **Integration**: Integrating human health education with biodiversity conservation messages.
*   **Waste & Zoonoses**: Addressing practical local issues like waste management which directly impact both wildlife (tigers) and humans.
