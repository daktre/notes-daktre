# Video Resource: Green Hub Dialogues - In Conversation with Dr. Prashanth NS

**Event**: Green Hub Dialogues
**Date**: April 29, 2020
**Role**: Featured Speaker

## Context
This conversation took place early in the COVID-19 pandemic. It focuses on the broader implications of the crisis, linking it to ecological health and human survival.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways

### 1. Health Problems as "Wicked Problems"
*   **Argument**: Pandemics are "wicked problems"—complex, interconnected issues that have no simple solution and require insights from multiple systems (political, social, biological).
*   **Takeaway**: Solving them requires "transdisciplinary" collaboration, not just medical expertise.

### 2. Investment in Public Health
*   **Argument**: The pandemic made visible the chronic under-investment in public health systems.
*   **Takeaway**: There is an urgent need to invest in "surveillance" systems—not just for viruses, but for the ecological drivers of disease.

### 3. The One Health Approach
*   **Argument**: Human well-being cannot be separated from the health of the planet.
*   **Takeaway**: Future pandemic preparedness must be ecological in nature ("Planetary Health").
