# Video Resource: Health Promotion for Well-being, Equity, and Sustainable Development

**Event**: Lancet Citizens' Commission on Reimagining India's Health System & HSTP Webinar
**Date**: April 2022
**Role**: Panelist

## Context
A high-level policy discussion on how to shift India's health system from a focus on "curing disease" to "promoting health."

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways

### 1. Reimagining Health Systems
*   **Argument**: The current system is reactive. A sustainable system must be proactive, focusing on preventing illness before it starts.
*   **Takeaway**: "Health promotion" is not just education; it's about creating environments (social, physical, economic) where healthy choices are possible.

### 2. Equity as the Compass
*   **Argument**: Health promotion strategies must not inadvertently widen the gap between rich and poor (e.g., advising expensive diets).
*   **Takeaway**: All interventions must be "equity-proofed."
