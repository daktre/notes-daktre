**Overall assessment of risk to participants as per ICMR human participation in research ethics guidelines 2017**
Minor increase over minimal risk or Low risk

**Major concerns**
- Proposal objecgtives indicate hypotheses related to socio-economic patterning of outcome variables, but the tools do not appear to collect any. PLease clarify. Are the tools submitted incomplete? 
- Proposal lacks important details of how human participants shall be selected. What is the sampling strategy, inclusion criteria, exclusions if any. 
- There are no details on how individual human participants shall be identified/recruited for the study
- Participant information sheet addresses ethics committee member rather than the participant (see for eg. section under risks & benefits)
- Benefits or lack thereof, if any to the participant have not been mentioned. This is a must in information sheets (check with institutional guidance)
- Participant info sheets are usually be in local language & should include contact details of the ethics committee secretary in case participants want to report violations (check institutional guidance on this)
- How will the researcher deal with low-literacy participants who may not be able to understand information sheets and consent procedures? Briefly explain/reflect 
- If participant were to receive identifying information in their secondary data/RTI queries, pelase include a section as to how that information shall be used/represented
- I see no mention of how the study findings will be communicated to study participants. While this might be tedious to do so to each participant, some thinking of how at least the region/demographic shall benefit from the study, even if it is a reflection/weakness/acknowledgement will be important. 
- There is no information on who will conduct the data collection, their capacity/training etc. Will it be the PI herself? 
- Final survey tool has not been included; please check with institutional/IEC SOP. Typically, IECs require final tools to be submitted for examining ethical concerns in the data being collected


**Minor/discretionary/suggestions**
- Note use of casualty & mortality which are synonymous terms as if they are separate (see for eg. p.5 of proposal)
- Relatively weak role for participants in shaping the research agenda; consider improving/increasing participatory methods that allow more local voices and give more agency to participants especially those who are experiencing impacts of conservation/HWI
