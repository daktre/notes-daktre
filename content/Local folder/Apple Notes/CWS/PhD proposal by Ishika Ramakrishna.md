**Overall assessment of risk to participants as per ICMR human participation in research ethics guidelines 2017**
Minimal risk

**Major concerns**

- Participant proposes stratifying by ethnicity, religion (“religious inclinations” is the term used which was not defined and hence I could not understand; is there a kind of religion mosaic/flexibility in this region?). Will final sample be proportional to population size of these strata? This is not only important from scientific point of view, but also from the view of inclusiveness of people, especially if some of the communities here are socially vulnerable/martginalised
- Since the researcher lists a permanent address in Bangalore, who shall be their local partner and what kind of local gatekeeping/community consent mechanisms have been thought of? 
- Participant info sheets should include contact details of the ethics committee secretary in case participants want to report violations (check institutional guidance on this)
- How will the researcher deal with low-literacy participants who may not be able to understand information sheets and consent procedures? Briefly explain/reflect 
- Benefits or lack thereof, if any to the participant have not been mentioned. This is a must in information sheets (check with institutional guidance)
- I see no mention of how the study findings will be communicated to study participants. While this might be tedious to do so to each participant, some thinking of how at least the region/demographic shall benefit from the study, even if it is a reflection/weakness/acknowledgement will be important. 
- Final tools have not been included; please check with institutional/IEC SOP. Typically, IECs require final tools to be submitted for examining ethical concerns in the data being collected


**Minor/discretionary/suggestions**
- Proposal categorises people who speak a given religion as a “cultural” group; please review
