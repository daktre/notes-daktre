HENI welcomes Indian organisations which are committed to improving research and action towards improving health equity in India. Bogth organisations and individuals are welcome to regular membership of HENI. The following non-exhaustive list of types of organisations are welcome:
- Academic/practitioner-based Public health/healthcare research organisations
- Activist networks/groups 
- Non-profit organisations involved in service delivery
 Membership to the steering group of HENI is limited to individuals only. 

An essential pre-requisite for membership shall be the affirmation of the HENI vision document by the organisational/network leadership to be communicated in writing to the HENI secretariat. Organisations with financial or collaborative ties to the tobacco, alcohol, food and pharmaceutical industries may not apply for HENI membership. Organisations are encouraged to consult with existing HENI member-organisations to facilitate a better understanding of HENI (see website for details). 

Procedure for institutional membership
1. Organisations that wish to become institutional members shall submit a letter addressed to the HENI steering group communicating: (a) confirm their registered status and work in India, (b) interest in becoming a member, (c) indicate agreement to the HENI vision as communicated in the vision document available on HENI website, (d) nominate a liaison from their organisation to receive communication and disseminate HENI-related information within their organisation, and (e) identify a current individual/organisational member who is willing to nominate/facilitate the membership. 
2. HENI steering group shall discuss membership annually and invite the liaison for discussions if needed. 
3. Membership shall be for a period of two years and renewed every two years.
