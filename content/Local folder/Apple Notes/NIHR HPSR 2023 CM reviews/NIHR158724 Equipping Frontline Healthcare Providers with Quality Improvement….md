Major comments
- COVID-19 and Lassa fever are together taken up with a focus on improving quality of healthcare services 
- Systematically planned steps with use of mixed methods in evalutating frontline health worker capacity strengthening 
- Limited engagement with the P & S in HPSR - policy & systems
- Relatively limited engagement with literature on why capacity strengthening alone may not work within specific LMIC health systems
- Relatively limited HPSR methods expertise in a team that is otherwise well qualified to undertake this quality improvement and capacity strengthening research

Minor comments
- Relatively limited engagement of health workers or communities/community voice and/or notions of quality prevalent among them

Overall
On Relevance of the proposed research, contextually relevant focus on LF & COVID-19, but with relatively limited policy and systems expertise or focus. 

On Research quality and excellence, while the proposal is indeed a well planned quality improvement/capacity strengthening research within health services, there is relatively limited engagement with various other health systems components (see for example limited role of governance, community engagement, health worker dynamics…)

On the Strength of the research team, well qualified team for capacity strengthening and quality research but with shortcomings for undertaking HPSR.

On Impact and sustainability, limited detail on how gains could sustain beyond the project or expand to newer geographies.
