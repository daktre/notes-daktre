Major comments
- Multi-country and multi-skilled team aiming to create a network for improved management of Dengue in multiple affected countries
- Strong focus on epidemiological, trials and clinical components with relatively weak focus on health poliyc and systems aspects
- Despite the importance of focus on Dengue, the proposal focuses exclusively on Dengue disease dynamics with insufficient focus on wider systems for febrile/infectious diseases in the affected countries

Minor comments
- WP2 & 3 are pre-dominantly epidemiologically focused with WP4 too largely focusing on clinical research (as opposed to wider social/community aspects of the disease)

Overall
On Relevance of the proposed research, the application does demonstrate relevance of the research to the affected countries but with single disease focus and limited engagement with social & wider systemic issues surrounding febrile and infectious diseases in these countries. 

On Research quality and excellence, while the quality of the clinical and epidemiological work proposed is high in quality, there is limited engagement with the policy/systems surrounding Dengue in these settings. Methods choices to reflect this.  

On the Strength of the research team, the team is multi-country and has complementary skills in conducting good quality clinical, epidemiological and capacity-building requirements for Dengue disease control and innovations. 
 
On Impact and sustainability, limited engagement with country health systems is a weakness.
