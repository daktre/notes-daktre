Major comments
- Learning health systems approach is well suited for the study, however, the link between obj 1 & 2 is weak. They both appear to have independent scope for being good research objectives. 
- Aim 1, rq (c) is framed in a yes/no way when in fact the proposal does acknowledge that there may in fact be many barriers even if community pharmacies are more accessible than other existing options
- Aim 2, rq(c) is the realist component of the study. In a realist component, typically an outcome needs to be defined so that the context and the mechanisms linked to a specific outcome chosen by the study may be examined. The outcome defined is “engagement (by professionals) in IR”. The chosen outcome “engagement” is not tangible enough for a realist evaluation and this needs to be carefully examined as the scientific output in Aim 2 depends on a good RE. 
- Weak conceptualisation and integration of the realist approach and appears to be limited expertise within the team in undertaking one, nor is there a mention of extenal collaboration that could fulfil this gap. Scope of RE though is well identified in their study albeit references list does not indicate familiarity/inclusion of the kind of realist evaluation studies nor methodological refs to realist studies.  
- The data analysis of Aim 2 appears to confuse the analysis of the realist component (which focuses on theorising) and the qual component (where they claim to use insider perspective to build a ToC). There is this ambiguity in the analytical approach in Aim 2. 
- Good ODA compliance with all activities being implemented in Ghana and Kenya


Minor comments
- CEI component could have been stronger given that Aim 2 involves extensive community-building

Overall
On Relevance of the proposed research, the project is strong on relevance as it is likely to result in improved understanding of the role of community pharmacies in two country settings in Africa. If well implemented it could indeed result in better access to medicines. 

On Research quality and excellence, the two objectives/aims have limited coherence and appear to be relatively independent of each other. The analytical approach proposed for the 2nd aim/objective that uses the realist approach has some shortcomings (see comments), 

On the Strength of the research team, good skill-mix in the team with a gap in experience in conducting realist inquiry but could be mitigated through collaboration

On Impact and sustainability, the community and learning health system component is likely to be crucial in the sustainability component, but appears poorly leveraged in the dissemination section (section 5). Some of the pathways to impact proposed while good are non-specific.
