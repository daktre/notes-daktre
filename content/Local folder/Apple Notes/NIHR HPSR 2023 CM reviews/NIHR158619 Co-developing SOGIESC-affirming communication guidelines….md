Major comments

- Well justified focus on SOGIESC views, norms and attitudes towards SOGIESC; the project focus on this is likely to be transformative in terms of health systems practice if indeed the project succeeds
- Comprehensive systems view with questions at macro-, meso- & micro-levels of the mental healthcare system in Malaysia
- Discourse analysis while important forms too central a component in the activities & outputs; other methods/approaches occupy relatively limited contribution to the outputs
- Justification for use of survey of 500 people to assess public perception is rather weak given that the attempt is not to create a generalisable assessment of public perception
- Very well conceptualised framework with clearly interacting components across macro, mesh & micro with research questions, methods & outputs
Minor comments
- Title under-emphasises the potential contributions of the proposal

On Relevance of the proposed research, the project is highly relevant and meaningfully integrates lived experiences of SOGIESC and attempts to create better policy and practice in mental healthcare delivery and in terms of practices at healthcar organisations
 
On Research quality and excellence, very appropriate use of discourse analysis albeit limited leveraging of the other research methods. 

On the Strength of the research team, the involvement of the NGO/CEI is very positive but greater scope for co-production exists. 

On Impact and sustainability, likely to sustain if successful. Good policy engagement strategies in place albeit success criteria are heavily project/activity focused rather than focusing on outcome/impacts.
