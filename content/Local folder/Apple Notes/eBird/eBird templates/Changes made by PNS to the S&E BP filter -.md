- Schoeniparus sp. - needed? (fulvetta sp exists, this seems to be a sub-set of that)
- **Set Marsh Babbler at 0. (only at western edge of filter in lower Dibang)**
- **Remove Indian Grassbird (Rufous-rumped Grassbird)**

**TREE-BABBLERS, SCIMITAR-BABBLERS, AND ALLIES**
- **Set Chestnut-capped Babbler to 0 (NL records?)**
- **Removed Rufous-throated Wren-babbler (Lower Dibang/Roing records?)**
- **Reduced Rusty-throated Wren-babbler (Mishmi WB). Very local distribution to E Arunachal & based on current records all being <5 in a list**
- **Bar-winged Wren-babbler reduced to 1. Only reported from western edge of filter in Mishmi hills/Lower Dibang region, and most records of single individuals**
- **Long-tailed (Naga) Wren-babbler reduced to 1; rarely more than 1 recorded and hyper-local distribution**
- **Chin Hills Wren-babbler reduced to 0. No validated records yet and hyper-local to southern Mizoram**
- **Tawny-breasted Wren-babbler Reduce number to 5 based on current records & restricted range within filter to specific areas in Meghalaya**
- **Chevron-breasted Babbler: reduce number to 2**
- **Red-billed, Coral-billed, Slender-billed, Streak-breasted, White-browed and Large Scimitar-Babbler numbers reduced to 5 based on current records, even from long travelling lists. Rarely more than 1 or 2 per list recorded.**
- **Removed Rusty-cheeked Scimitar-Babbler**
- **Increase Spot-breasted Scimitar-Babbler to 2**
- **Snowy-throated Babbler increase from 0 to 2**
- **Reduce Blackish-breasted (Sikkim Wedge-billed) Babbler to 0. Range restricted in filter to Lower Dibang**

**WHITE-EYES, YUHINAS, AND ALLIES**
- **No changes**

**PARROTBILLS, WRENTIT, AND ALLIES**
- **Removed Yellow-eyed Babbler**
- **Reduced Jerdon’s Babbler to 0. Restricted range in lower Dibang**
- **Removed Great Parrotbill, Brown Parrotbill**
- **Reduced Grey-headed Parrotbill, White-breasted (Greater Rufous-headed Parrotbill) to 20**
- **Removed Rufous-headed Parrotbill,** ***Psittiparus bakeri*** **(but possible in Eastern Arunachal?). It was currently set at 0.**
- **Increase Spot-breasted Parrotbill to 5**
- **Reduce Fulvous Parrotbill to 5. Range restricted to lower Dibang on this filter**
- **Reduce Black-throated Parrotbill number to 20 in line with current records and other higher Parrotbill numbers.**
