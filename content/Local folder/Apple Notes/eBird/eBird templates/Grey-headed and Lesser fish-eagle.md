As you know till recently, the only fish-eagle species in southern India was the Grey-headed Fish-Eagle. However, in 2004 when the fish-eagles around Cauvery WLS were closely examined, they all turned out to be the Lesser Fish-Eagles (see documentation of this by Nick Latheby here: <a href="http://speciesguide.delhibird.net/internal/17/eagle_lesser_fish.htm" rel="noopener" class="external-link" target="_blank"><u>http://speciesguide.delhibird.net/internal/17/eagle_lesser_fish.htm</u></a>). Since then, there has been a revision of ids by photographers and birdwatchers of Fish-eagles seen around Cauvery WLS. As per my knowledge, the most commonly occurring fish-eagle around this location is the Lesser Fish-Eagle (albeit the Grey-headed Fish-eagle is known from nearby locations in BRT hills and reservoirs nearby). Considering this, could you please provide additional information about this observation? This could include the circumstances of the sighting, description of relevant field characteristics, calls, behaviour, notes on how similar species were eliminated and, if possible, a photograph or video. In the lack of sufficient documentation to establish Grey-headed Fish-Eagle, you could consider editing the observation to make it Lesser/Grey-headed Fish-Eagle.
