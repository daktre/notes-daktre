As you know the among the two of the Aquila spotted eagles that occur in our region (Indian Spotted Eagle and the Greater Spotted Eagle), the Greater Spotted Eagle is more partial to large waterbodies and is more commonly recorded, especially in winter. In general, while reporting these eagles, it would be useful to include as much notes as possible on the identification. A photograph, if available, should be included.
