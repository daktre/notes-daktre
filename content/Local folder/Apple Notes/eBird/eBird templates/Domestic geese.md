This is just to inform you that you could also consider using the "Domestic goose sp. (Domestic type) (Anser sp. (Domestic type))" in cases where you are sure that the bird seen is a domestic one. Goose sp. refers usually to free-living/feral populations of geese (such as unidentified geese or other Anser sp./hybrids which are free-living). 

This is for your information. Feel free to edit it if this was the case.
