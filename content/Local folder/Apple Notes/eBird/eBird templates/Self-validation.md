Per email to eBird-Karnataka reviewers, a list of spp. that are very well documented from BRH and are seen very often are allowed for self-validation by PNS. This is because the BRT region has temporarily been included in the KA-plains filter. These spp. will not get flagged once the new filter goes up (in progress). The spp. are:

Malabar Parakeet
Orange Minivet
Vernal Hanging-Parrot
Greater Flareback
Bronzed Drongo
Racket-tailed Drongo
Velvet-fronted Nuthatch
Yellow-browed Bulbul
Rufous Babbler
Southern Hill Myna
Nilgiri Flowerpecker
