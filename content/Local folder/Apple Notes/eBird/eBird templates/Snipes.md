Could you please provide additional information about this observation? As you know Snipe is a particularly difficult group to identify to species level, especially between Pin-tailed and Common Snipes. Hence, including all documentation on this record would be very useful. This could include the circumstances of the sighting, description of relevant field characteristics, calls, behaviour, notes on how similar species were eliminated and, if possible, a photograph or video. If sufficient documentation to identify to species is not available, you may always consider using snipe sp. (if enough documentation exists to identify the species as a Snipe), or further appropriate "slashes" such as Common/Pin-tailed Snipe or Pin-tailed/Swinhoe's Snipe may also be considered.
