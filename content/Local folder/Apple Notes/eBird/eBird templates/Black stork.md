Black Storks are recorded from very few locations in southern India. Your record could add a lot of value to the known distribution of this species. Hence, could you please provide additional information about this observation, especially on how the bird was identified and how it was told apart from other more common storks such as the Wooly-necked Stork? This could include the circumstances of the sighting, description of relevant field characteristics, calls, behaviour, notes on how similar species were eliminated and, if possible, a photograph or video.
