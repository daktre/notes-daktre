As you may know, wild Mallards are winter visitors to our parts. Most of them winter in the northern parts of the country. Further south especially in and around Bangalore, wild populations of Mallards are rare. However, many domesticated Mallards are often seen near tanks and waterbodies. These may be recorded under Mallard (Domestic type) in your eBird checklist. See for example, this checklist from Yediyur lake with photos of domestic Mallard. https://ebird.org/ebird/view/checklist?subID=S27467201

Many more photographs of domestic Mallards reported by eBird users, for your reference is here: https://ebird.org/media/india/catalog?taxonCode=mallar2&region=India%20(IN)&regionCode=IN&q=Mallard%20(Domestic%20type)%20-%20Anas%20platyrhynchos%20(Domestic%20type)
