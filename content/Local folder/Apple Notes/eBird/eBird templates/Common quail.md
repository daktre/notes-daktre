There are very few records of this species when compared to the more commonly seen bush-quails for example and hence your record is indeed important, if well documented. Hence, could you please provide additional information about this observation? This could include the circumstances of the sighting, description of relevant field characteristics, calls, behaviour, notes on how similar species were eliminated and, if possible, a photograph or video.
