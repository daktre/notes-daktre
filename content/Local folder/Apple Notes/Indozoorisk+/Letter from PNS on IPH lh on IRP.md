Madam/Sir,

Sub: Regarding our previous and ongoing collaborations with Vivekananda Girijana Kalyana Kendra, BR Hills, Karnataka

On behalf of the health equity cluster at IPH, Bengaluru (IPH), I am happy to share our experience with previous and ongoing research and community health projects that we implement in collaboration with the Vivekananda Girijana Kalyana Kendra. We have worked closely with VGKK since 2012 onwards when I was involved with supporting the setting up of community health and research activities at the VGKK hospital and community health unit at the time. VGKK is very well known in Karnataka state and in India for its pioneering work with the Soliga Adivasi (indigenous) people living in and around forested locations in southern Karnataka and neighbouring states. They have been a strong proponent of an integrated model of tribal development encompassing health, education, livelihood security, biodiversity conservation and sustainable development of tribal communities. Their hospital, community health engagement and educational infrastructure, for decades was the only facilities of its kind in these remote regions. Lately, they have set up extensive collaborations with the SOliga people and community-based organisations and have collaborated with local government and district administration in setting up long-term initiatives for the Soliga people. 

In 2014, Dr. Tanya Seshadri who heads the community health component at VGKK invited me to join her in an implementation research project titled XXXX. This was a participatory action research project that involved collaborations with a wide range of actors including academia, decision-makers/implementers from government health services at the district level, state health officials and local communities. The project was selected through a global competitive call for proposals and was one among xxx projects of its kind across the world funded by the Implementation Research Platform of the WHO Alliance for Health Policy & Systems Research, Geneva (USD XXXX). For a period of XX months from XXX 2014, IPH worked closely with Dr. Tanya Seshadri at VGKK in co-producing interventions to improve maternal health of local communities. The collaboration set up under this project has continued to expand into various other areas including non-communicable diseases, health policy and systems research and other areas and more recently has resulted in a long-term plan to set up a Centre for Research in Tribal Health (grant proposal in review). In 2017, when I was awarded a DBT/Wellcome Trust India Alliance Fellowship, our collaboration with VGKK enabled the setting up of a public health research field station in BR Hills, that continues to support long-term research on tribal health inequities (ongoing) under the “Towards Health Equity & Transformatige Action on Tribal Health” (THETA) project. Under THETA project, IPH has set up a field station on premises provided by VGKK and has also begun building capacity of VGKK for undertaking systematic research projects. 

VGKK is a embedded within the community with whom it works and has integrated several local beneficiaries of its program into its own governance, in keeping with its motto of empowering local communities. Since its inception in 1981, it has worked with a variety of international, national and local organisations and has kept high financial and administrative standards. We have had a very positive experience in setting up MoUs, co-organising research and project activities and procuring/mainteance of equipment in course of our collaborations. Since they are not a typical resaerch/academic organisation, their administrative and financial set-up is different from ours, but through our long-term collaboration we have been able to set up procedures that enable compliance with internal and statutory requirements at both these organisations. In the upcoming project too, I am sure VGKK shall manage the administrative and financial commitments in line with the standards that the consortium shall set.  
I wish the organisation the best in taking forward this grant.   

Dr. Prashanth N Srinivas, MBBS, MPH, PhD
