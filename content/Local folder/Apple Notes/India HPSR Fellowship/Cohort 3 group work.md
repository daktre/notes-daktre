**Group 2**
You are invited to provide technical assistance by the health department of AB state. They ask if you can help them improve the quality of care for people with non-communicable diseases, particularly Diabetes & Hypertension at PHCs in one of the districts, Gambhirgarh. 

The district is towards the northern edge of the state and is about a day’s journey from the capital by road. It has a population of 8,00,000 and is considered as having the least HDI in AB. It is also the constituency of the new health minister who is keen to improve quality of care in Gambhirgarh’s PHCs. Hence, he has posted a DC/DM who was originally trained MBBS and he has also organised a DHO to be chosen who has recently completed a 1 year PG diploma in public health at the state capital. 

You are the team expected to strengthen health systems of Gambhirgarh so that quality of care for people seeking care for NCDs at PHCs of the Gambhirgarh will improve. The health department says “we dont want just training and research; we want to strengthen the system itself”. How will you proceed?

**Group 3**
You are invited to provide technical assistance by the health department of AB state. They ask if you can help them improve the quality of care for people with non-communicable diseases, particularly Diabetes & Hypertension at PHCs in one of the districts, Gambhirgarh. 

The district is towards the northern edge of the state and is about a day’s journey from the capital by road. It has a population of 8,00,000 and is considered as having the least HDI in AB. It is also the constituency of the new health minister who is keen to improve quality of care in Gambhirgarh’s PHCs. Hence, he has posted a DC/DM who was originally trained MBBS and he has also organised a DHO to be chosen who has recently completed a 1 year PG diploma in public health at the state capital. 

The district has a large population of migrant labourers who work in several private mining areas, many of them remote and forested. The migrants live in temporary shelters. The district also has a large number of unlicensed medical practitioners who appear to be the first port of call for many people for checking their BP and sugar levels. A very important highway connecting two big metros passes through the district. Apart from mining, the second common activity is bidi rolling as a small-scale industry. 

You are based in a local academic organisation/NGO/medical college and you wish to undertake HPSR to really understand and improve quality of care with a focus on NCDs in PHCs. Assuming your organisation is providing you a small grant to do this, how will you go about planning this HPSR study?
