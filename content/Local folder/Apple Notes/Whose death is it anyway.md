Vaccine hesitancy being reported in some communities is not only about misinformation and illiteracy. These are perverse manifestations of the depleting trust in administration and public services. 

In a remote Adivasi hamlet inside the forests of the lush BIligirirangan hills tiger reserve in southern Karnataka, a Minister no less with an entourage of officers from the district administration (including myself) were brusquely shouted out of the home and front porch of an elderly Solega Adivasi woman. With all humility and good intentions, here was the Karnataka Minister of Education, Suresh Kumar, inviting her to protect her own health by walking few 10s of meters to the vaccine booth. The doctor from the not-so-nearby primary health center was today standing at the otherwise forsaken building, the residential school built in this remote hamlet nestled among forested hills of the region. Yet, the woman would have none of it. Admonishing the entourage of entering spaces she considered sacred, she packed off the delegation onto the next house and the neighbourhood, where more of the same was seen.
