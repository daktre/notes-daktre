**IITD proposal**
Very well written proposal and one of the few with urban primary health care focus. Few suggestions and comments:
1. In the challengs, there is limited information on the wider system challenges beyond what the technology can fix. Even if it is not taken up now, the formative phase will have to assess the ability of the local health system being studied to “act” on the more efficient decision-support system that the DHI is providing. Some insight-building into that is needed and should be anticipated. 
2. Objective has a relatilvely narrow tech-orientation/focus. the fact that well designed AI can produce health trend predictions appears to be the main focus/exploration. However, given the health systems focus of this research, the team could go a bit further to see if such trends spur/trigger action/decisions that in turn result in early action/preparation etc. What faciltiates its impact, and what impedes it…these systems cahllegnes that are beyond the capabilities of the tech/DHI need to be foregrounded in the objective
3. COrrespondingly the research questions need to be formulated as questions - preferably how/why questions given teh interdisciplinary mixed methods being proposed. RQ1 scope too could be broadened as per comment above: assessment of efficiency of the DHI could be broadened to the ability of the system to improve decision-making based on the capabilities of the DHI?
4. In the methodology, the action element and the efficiency mapping element of the DHI are well explained. however, there is limited information on the mixed methods coponent: what will the be the qual component about? How will it add to/alignn with the quant? Potentially, should the team just use a facilitators & barriers approach to see how the DHI is (a) received/perceived, (b) integrated into the workflow of the staff, (c) results in improved decision-making…..consider adding some logic model/framework as to what you expect to chagne in these facilities when the DHI is deployed and qual component can then study what happened…why etc? (this is just a suggestion).
