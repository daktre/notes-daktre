Is there any influence of your research on health policy or healthcare practice? If yes, please specify
There are multiple policy and practice influences potentially occurring through our work. 
At the community level, the impacts we see include
- Elevation of health access in the Sangha
- THETA project intervention’s focus on involving young tribal youth in sports for health promotion has triggered renewed interest at the Panchayat & district level.
- Invited to advice Government of Karnataka for designing of interventions to improve access to healthcare for tribal communities. Produced a background document for the action plan submitted by Govt of Karnataka ST welfare department to the National level tribal sub-plan that includes inputs from THETA project
- Focused policy engagement pilot taken up in collaboration with IA Fellow, Devaki Nambiar under a Wellcome Trust funded project titled ADEQUATE. In this project, 2 Adivasi youth from the Solega and the Koraga community have been working systematically at district and state level respectively in providing technical assistance to improve adaptation of existing ST welfare schemes to Adivasi populations. Many innovative practices have come out of this and this project requires systematic documentation of its own to map all the policy and practice impacts. 
- THETA project gave inputs to a new project funded by the ICMR National Task Force on NCDs which chose our field site as one of the sites for a multi-country study on how to improve NCD care for tribal populations. One of THETA project RAs received his first ever research grant from ICMR for this 3 year study
- Due to our strong community-based and participatory work, we were invited by the district administration during the COVID-19 2nd wave to join the district task force to provide special focus on protecting Adivasi communities in our district. Through the task force, several project insights from our work as well as from our Co-Investigator from teh Solega community were channelised. Some of these led to resepectful community engagement with respect to COVID-19 care in the tribal community and ongoing efforts to engage them meaningfully for vaccination as well. A separate documentation may be needed to map out the several policy and practice impacts of this piece of work.

Please describe briefly about how your research will ultimately improve the health outcomes for people 
Project has both direct and indirect benefits. 
Direct benefits to health outcomes include:
- clinical (weekly) NCD clinics that began in 2019 addressing NCDs and addictions in our collaborator tribal hospital
- Healthcare navigator intervention being piloted at VGKK hospital under THETA intervention
- Widespread COVID-19 awareness meetings held in collaboration with the Soliga Abhivruddhi Sangha during first and 2nd wave; more recently targeting vaccination
- Direct advocacy and technical assistance being provided to the district in culturally safe and immediate COVID-19 care facilities in tribal areas in Chamarajanagar 
- Our team was involved in emergency crisis mangement response by setting up triage and providing direct clinical care in remote taluka and smaller PHCs/hospitals in remote areas in the district
- Local language and culturally adapted awareness material design

Indirect impact is through 2 interventions:
- One intervention ongoing to establish hamlet-level sports & wellness clubs targeting addiction and health promotion of tribal youth. 9 clubs are active and ongoing
- District level intervention in examining role of forest rights on health ongoing.

Public Engagement Programmes Undertaken. Please indicate if you participated in/undertook public engagement activitiesMulti Line Text. 
Being a communiuty-embedded work, we have extensive public engagement activities ranging from community/public events to large-scale events. 
- PE grant for setting up a planetary health information center based on our findings from NE Indian site is ongoing and will result in establishment of a participatory planetary health interpretation center in Pakke Tiger Reserve in Arunachal Pradesh in partnership with a collaborating Ecologist from Srishti School of Design, Bangalore & Arunachal Pradesh Forest Department
- 4 leadership workshops for Solega and other Adivasi youth from multiple locations in southern Karnataka was held benefitting over a 100 Adivasi youth over the last year
- Doddasampige Adivasi Library set up in Bangalay Podu as an intervention is also a PE activity. Over 30 children and young adults have been using the library with computer/Internet facility for health education and recreation
- Our interventions (Sports & wellness clubs) is as much an intervention as it is a PE activity. Under this intervention, we have held 3 tournaments for Adivasi youth across the district where over a 100 Adivasi youth have participated till date
- Karnataka Rugby Federation did a solidarity and team-building exchange workshop between state rugby players and tribal youth where over 60 people participated
- As PI, I have attended over 10 meetings at schools, colleges and in the community for various events including International Women’s Day which was specially organised with project support among Solega women leaders, for the first time in this community. 
- As PI, I also join the local Gram Panchayat COVID-19 Task Force and meet them every month for the last year advising on how to manage COVID-19 in our village
