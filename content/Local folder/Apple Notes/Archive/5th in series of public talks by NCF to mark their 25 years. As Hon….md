5th in series of public talks by NCF to mark their 25 years. As Hon associate of NCF…..



Nitin Rai is a political ecologist and has been a Fellow at the Ashoka Trust for Research in Ecology and the Environment (ATREE), Bangalore, India. He studies the impacts of conservation and development on communities and local resistance to state efforts. His research incorporates ecological science, history of landscape transformation, cultural ecology and local knowledge. From what I have seen of his work, the role of “power” and role of “knowledge” (how it is produced, who wields and uses it…etc) have been central to his work. In line with the political ecology lens that he wields, his work also aims to challenge current narratives on how nature is used, by whom and questions like this. Lot of his work has been in partnership with the Solega community and Adivasi social movements to study the politics of conservation in a protected area in South India….which is where I had the opportunity to watch and learn from some of his work….
