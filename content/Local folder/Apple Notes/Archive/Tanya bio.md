Dr. Tanya Seshadri has been with the Vivekananda Girijana Kalyana Kendra, BR Hills, Karnataka since 2012. She has been instrumental in setting up the Tribal Health Resource Center and is involved with overseeing the hospital and community health program catering to the Solega and other Adivasi communities in Chamarajnagar district. The center is also involved in participatory action research and collaborative research on maternal and child health, sickle cell disease and haemoglobinopathies, zoonotic diseases and in addressing other health problems among Adivasi communities. She has also been a community health consultant in tribal health research projects implemented by the Achutha Menon Centre for Health Science Studies at the Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute of Science & Technology, Trivandrum. Earlier she has been involved in setting up community health initiatives at the Christian Institute of Health Science Research, Dimapur, Nagaland and in health systems strengthening initiatives at district level in Tumkur and Chamarajanagar districts. She has consulted on health inequities and social determinants of health initiatives with leading international and national organisations including the WHO Alliance for Health Policy & Systems Research, Geneva and the National Health Systems Resource Centre, New Delhi. Since 2017, she has been involved in incubating the Health Equity Network India which brings together several public health, social science and civil society academia for advancing research and knowledge for addressing health inequities. She is currently leading the establishment of a multi-institutional research and training center for action on tribal health called the Center for Training, Research & Innovation in Tribal Health under an award from the DBT/Wellcome Trust India Alliance.
