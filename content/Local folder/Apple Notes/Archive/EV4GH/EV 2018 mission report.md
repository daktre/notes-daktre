I participated in the Emerging Voices for Global Health 2018 that was held at the Liverpool School of Tropical Medicine, Liverpool UK. As an alumnus of ITM (MPH and PhD programs), as well as being an alumnus of the EV training program as well (EV 2010 cohort), I am grateful to the ITM alumni funding opportunity to be able to participate in the event. 

My participation at the event fulfilled the following responsibilities:

1. I was heading various functions related to oversight of logistics preparation, scheduling, facilitation and conduct of the EV face-to-face program. I arrived in Liverpool earlier than other members  (on Sep 24) in order to supervise the preparations. Immediately upon my arrival on I had to have meetings with Laura Dean (from Liverpool School) and coordinate preparations towards food, check on accommodation arrangements and organise cash for immediate reimbursements etc. First few days were devoted mainly to logistics. After the initial 2 days devoted to logistics, my colleagues from the IPH EV secretariat joined me and we had series of meetings to finalise the preparations to welcoming the EVs and the EV facilitators. We organised the first EV facilitators meeting on 27 September where about 15 or so of the EV facilitators and secretariat members met. The agenda for the Facilitators meeting is as follows:
**Chaired by Dorcus**
*Co-facilitated by Laura and Prashanth*

1. Status of EV’s and facilitators arrival - *Ketki Shah*
2. On-site preparations at LSTM (classrooms, break-out rooms, poster display, lunch and snacks arrangements) - *Laura Dean*
3. Status of accommodation and rooming arrangements of EVs and facilitators - *Annelies & Ketki*
4. Registration, welcome kit and per-diems - *Ketki and Pragati*
5. Food arrangements (Breakfast, lunch & dinner) - *Laura & Pragati*
6. Health systems visit day preparations - *Laura*
7. Poster day preparations - *Laura*

10. Daily Morning meeting (to review preparations for the day) & evening facilitator meeting (for reviewing how the day went and to prep for the next day) - *Faraz*


The EV F2F program began on 28 September and ended on 6th October with a young researcher pre-conference at Liverpool School. I participated each day and was involved in overseeing the daily preparations in coordination with the Chair of EV4GH (I was the co-chair of the governance and the lead member from the EV secretariat). Each day, I was present at the venue full day and participated in the teaching and facilitation. I was also involved with being a group facilitator for a thematic group of EVs and was involved in guiding them on the thematic topic. 

For the pre-conference, I was involved in preparing the agenda as well as the point-person for one of the thematic groups. 

On the day of the pre-conference, we had a meeting with the EV 2020 local organising committee head who was visiting from Dubai. This was related to the preparations for EV 2020. 

From 8th to 12th October, I attended the 5th symposium on HSR at Liverpool. I attended all days. I was involved in a few sessions: (1) Emerging Voices debate on Day 3, (2) Poster based on my study (Day 2), (3) Invited to facilitate the general body meeting of Health Systems Global as a member of HSG, (Day 3). I participated in sessions related to primary health care, tribal health and was actively involved in covering the deliberations at the event on Twitter (Tweets are publicly accessible under handle @prashanthns). 

On October 13th, I facilitated a debrief of all EVs and obtained feedback on the program. After the half day debrief, I had a half day meeting with the secretariat to close the accounts and make preparations to leave. 

October 14th onwards, I was on official leave for personal travels and returned to Bangalore on Oct 19.
