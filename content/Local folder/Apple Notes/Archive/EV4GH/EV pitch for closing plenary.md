Advancing health systems for all requires concerted action not only at global and national levels, but at much smaller and less well understood arenas. We are talking about populations, geographies, cultures, languages and ideas that are not a part of the global and national mainstreams, and hence are sidelined passively, or even pushed away actively through more powerful social, economic and political forces. 

The Emerging Voices for Global Health seeks to engage critically with these global and national level narratives by bringing fresher, younger and critical ideas to engage with the broader agenda-setting that happens at higher levels. This edition too, 30 young people selected from among nearly 700 aspirants came together in an intensive 2-week programme preceding the symposium. Before that, these 30 new entrants into the Health Systems Global membership engaged with the theme of advancing health systems for all via an online distance learning programme. 

During the closing plenary, one of the alumni of the Emerging Voices group will offer a young health systems researcher perspective. In course of the panel submission, the EV alumnus chosen will especially engage with the following themes:
- How inclusive has the symposium been in terms of addressing the various themes brought up by the young EV fellows this year?
- How can younger voices and especially voices from LMICs become stronger and impactful within the global health community
- From a young researcher perspective, what are some of the rays of hope in moving forward towards advancing health systems for all
