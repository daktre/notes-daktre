To, 

The Royal Society of Tropical Medicine and Hygiene

Dear Sir/Madam,

I write this letter in support of the grant application introduced by Manoj Kumar Pati. I have been approached to be the local supervisor for a PhD proposal that he is putting together to begin a study on how to improve care for non-communicable diseases in primary care. India has a large burden of people suffering from NCDs. However, our primary health care systems are not geared to provide continuous care needed for NCDs due to various health system deficiencies. There is a huge need to support government health systems as most poor in India depend on care provided in government hospitals. 

For his PhD and in this proposed project, Manoj is hoping to improve our understanding of how PHCs may be strengthened to provide continuous care for NCDs (Diabetes and Hypertension). He proposes to use realist evaluation for this purpose. By focusing efforts on how certain programs work in specific contexts and not everywhere, and on trying to understand why they tend to work for some and not for others, realist evaluation helps generate evidence that can directly be relevant for practice and policy. Realist evaluation is a relatively new concept in many LMICs and having trained extensively and having published on realist evaluation in Indian settings, I am really looking forward to the results of Manoj’s work. 

I know Manoj for about five years now since he began working at Institute of Public Health Bengaluru. When I met him, he was a reserach assistant on another project and I saw that he is very hardworking. He applied to work under my supervision in a research project on access to medicines. In my project he led the quantitative analysis of a large household survey dataset and was also involved in various qualitative data collection and analysis. The project is now complete and Manoj is in the process of preparing papers for submission. 

I am happy to support Manoj’s proposal for undertaking this study and I hope it will also help him launch a successful career as a public health researcher. I have no doubts that he will be able to undertake this project successfully if it were to come thorugh.   Yours truly,
 Prashanth N S
