I am delighted to write a letter of recommendation for Sabu K U for the upcoming edition of the Atlantic Fellowship. I know Sabu for a about two years as a PhD student working under the supervision of a close collaborator of mine at the Sree Chitra Tirunal Institute for Medical Sciences and Technology in Trivandrum. I got to know him in course of seminars and other academic events for which I have been visiting the Institute in course of my work on health inequalities in India. From the very first time I met him, Sabu struck me as an inquisitive and critical thinker. I was impressed by Sabu’s perseverance in advancing dialogue and discussion on health inequalities. He never naively accepted textbook descriptions and popular explanations and instead advanced the discussions deeper. 

In course of his PhD, Sabu has worked in remote and forested areas in northern Kerala. Sadly, during his PhD fieldwork, disaster struck (floods) and Sabu responded immediately with coordinating and helping with humanitarian assistance to grassroots workers on ground immediately. He has developed close relationships with the people on ground and I find that he’s as comfortable in these settings, as he is in academia. 

Sabu is soft-spoken yet firm in his acknowledgment of how health inequalities are systematically affecting large populations. I am sure Sabu will continue to do meaningful and socially relevant research/academic work on health inequalities. I believe the fellowship will be an excellent opportunity for Sabu to nurture his interests, gain useful networks as well as professionally advance in his career, so that he may make an impact on health inequalities in this region. I wholeheartedly recommend him for this fellowship.
