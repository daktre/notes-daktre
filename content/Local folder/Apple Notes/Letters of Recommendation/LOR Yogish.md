I am delighted to write a letter of recommendation for Yogish Channa Basappa for the upcoming short course on multivariable analysis at Institute of Tropical Medicine, Antwerp.  
I know Yogish for over five years. As a fresh student of Masters in Public Health from one of India’s leading Universities (Manipal University), Yogish interned for a month at a forest area where IPH has a field station that works on tribal health. His keen interest in working in geographically and socially challenging settings motivated him to intern with us at this forest area field station. Subsequently, he has spent about a year in remote areas of Northeast India with MSF. In January 2018, he applied to join my research team and has since then taken primary responsibility for designing and implementing surveys in challenging and remote tribal areas of the country. He has conducted the surveys with great attention to detail and ensuring rigorous data quality. Currently, he is leading the quantitive data analysis task within our team and is keen to hone his skills in multi-variate analysis. We hope to be able to work on a mixed effects model that explains the contribution of geographical remoteness and social factors in determining poor health outcomes and malnutrition in tribal populations in different parts of India. 

Yogish is hard-working and is already quite competent in using SPSS as an advanced user and has begun experimenting with coding using R. I am sure this workshop will greatly benefit Yogish professionally as well as improve our team’s ability to bring out high quality results in tribal health surveys. He also has a reasonably good grasp on conceptual foundations in biostatistics and this workshop comes at a right time in his professional career.   Based on this, I strongly recommend Yogish for this short course and am happy to provide any further clarifications if needed.
