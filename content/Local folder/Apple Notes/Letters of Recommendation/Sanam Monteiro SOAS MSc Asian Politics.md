I write this letter of recommendation for Sanam  Martins Braga Monteiro, who is applying for the full time MSc programme in Asian Politics at your institution. 

Sanam has been under my supervision for her internship at Institute of Public Health, Bangalore since January 2018. In course of these few months, I am impressed with her motivation to engage learn about issues around health inequities in the Indian context, especially with respect to indigenous tribal health. Sanam is an active listener and is easily able to engage critically with the literature she is given to read on the topic, as well as participate in an informed manner in seminars, discussions and debates that we have on these topics. I also find her to be an independent learner and is able to work on tasks related to literature review and synthesis, writing and readings tasks related to the topic of research independently. She is confident and clear in her written and oral communication. 

Under the internship, she was allotted the task of mapping various theories in various disciplines of the social sciences, related to marginalisation and social exclusion of indigenous people. She has progressed with this task admirably and has also gone ahead and contextualised these theories for the Indian context. Her ability to read, understand and critically engage with new material is impressive. 

In her work right now, she is working closely with interns from other countries including Nepal and Indian students. She is able to work in close coordination with them in the team and I find her to be very open to new learning tasks as well as new cultural experiences including food and other recreational activities. She has demonstrated a passion for undertaking desk research as well as fieldwork in limited resource conditions. During the internship, she has been posted to our field station in a remote district, where she managed the allotted tasks independently. 

She is a very warm and cheerful person and a keen learner. She is quite articulate in spoken and written English. I wish her the very best in her higher academic pursuit and warmly and strongly recommend her for this programme, given her academic background and interest in Asian studies.
