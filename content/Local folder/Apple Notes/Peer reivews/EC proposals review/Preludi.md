**C1: Excellence**
- The relation to the work programme, while relevant is not detailed out to a sufficient degree, identifying alignments with the GACD objectives beyond making an assertion that it is related (section 1.2). 
- Objectives while within scope do not immediately identify innovative or SOA approaches being employed. The idea of promoting positive mental health/mental health promotion as opposed to mental health care delivery or strengthening or other public health intervention is indeed innovative. 
- Proposal is strong on concepts and foundations (positive mental health, social capital, building resilience and OD approach). However, the application of these concepts into activities at the level of WPs lacks the detailing out needed to make a good determination of its feasibility and impact (especially WP2 and WP3). 
- Project ambition section is very scant beyond a blanket assertion on its skills and potential
- Involvement of adolescents more so through working with schools is a strength. 
- Application in remote areas possibly involves neglected populations
 

**C2: Impact**
- Several potential impacts of the project can be foreseen. However, the entire section 2 of the proposal that is supposed to deal with impact does not identify specific impact areas. The first part of section 2.1 (expected impact) describes project activities and identifies strengths of the OD approach rather than identifying discrete impacts. 
- In the third paragraph, project monitoring/evaluation points are mentioned and project outputs/outcomes are identified along with mention of tools and methodological details. 
- Based on information given, it was difficult to make a determination of the impact although the project *does have* such a potential and several possible impact pathways exist, they have not been spelt out at all in the relevant sections. 

**C3: Quality**
- Involvement of institutions and partners who are familiar with the approach and are practitioners
- Consortium partners are well chosen and are likely to benefit from and implement the intervention well. 
- Work packages are not uniformly described in detail allowing for a good determination of the feasibility. For example, WP4 provides sufficient detail about the methods and implementation, but not WP2 and 3 would have benefitted from lot more details given that the main proposal body did not provide enough methodological details related to the overall project. 

**Overall**
This is an excellent “idea” conceptually involving action in educational institutions, and among adolescents, seeking to promote mental health. The set of partners also have the capacity to implement such an intervention and the methods chosen are appropriate. Despite several positives, the proposal in the current form lacks a description of impact as required in the call (and evaluation criteria) and it does not explicitly identify impacts in a way that allows for a good determination on its merit. On the other two criteria of excellence and quality, the proposal is well rated. Based on information provided under Impact, an assessment has also been made.
