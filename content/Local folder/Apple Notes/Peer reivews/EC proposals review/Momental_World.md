**C1: Excellence**
Objectives are clearly stated. O1 provides a powerful way of comparing and analysing treatment process and outcomes across several settings and mental disorders in participating institutions. Of the objectives, O1 addresses implementation, while O2 addresses the technology and O3 addresses policy. Well balanced objectives across a wide range of settings (HIC and LMIC settings). Sub-objectives under O3 appear to not be fulfilled by the activities/WPs, or are perhaps framed too ambitiiously/broadly (for eg. reducing stigma and social exclusion is beyond the scope of O3 activities/WPs but it is a listed specific objective under O3). Relationship to the work programme is well stated. The synergies with various other initiatives/projects listed on p.5 is very positive, but unclear as to how such synergies are going to be built (for eg. a project such as IMAGEMEND - how specifically will it contribute to Momental_world is unclear). While such a synergy intent is very positive, lack of specific alignment details with so many listed projects make it difficult to assess if this is a very widely stated intention only or if the Momental_World has specifically planned beyond such an intent. Overall, the coherence aacross 4 phases of the projects, its objectives and the stated outcomes is very good, and the focus on identifying at least 1800 vulnerable patients with mental disorders across 7 countries is both ambitious and demonstrated well in the WPs. 

**C2: Impact**
- The model to be identified in Phase 2 could identify structural problems outside the direct control of country health servcies or mental health care systems thus imposing difficulties in actually validating/realising the benefits of such problem identification or modelling. This aspect is poorly identified/acknowledged and I dont see any specific mitigation strategies if that is the case. 
- Many outcomes are stated/framed for European settings. Sometimes, it is unclear as to what the benefit to LMIC partners would be and how their participation will add value to the European settings and/or to their own. (for eg. the bold text on p.17). Although the contribution of cross-country comparison is well described in p.20 & p.21, the framing of benefits only to European settings is troublesome. Also, given the coplexity of contexts even within participating countries, Momental_world impact may not be generalisable to any/many of the participating countries, although the general impact at the level of European settings/health systems may be more feasible than the expectation of any policy impact in the participating LMIC health systems. 

**C3: Quality**
The consortium strengths and the workplans appear quite feasible. The detailing out of the activities allows for a good assessment of the efficiency of implementation. 
- WP2 on evaluation is weak on benefitting from the diverse implementation contexts. No details on how treatment process comparisons will be made and how country health system contexts shaping care pathways and outcomes will be considered and compared and how the WP2 (evaluation) will contribute to the model is unclear. 

**Overall**
