**C1: Excellence**
- Well formulated primary and secondary objectives, with clear links made between objectives and specific activities (WPs)
- Choice of low-cost interventions among disadvantaged populations within the economically worse-off European countries addresses equity considerations very well. 
- Systematic manner of starting with adaptation and contextualisation and proceeding towards high quality evidence using RCTs
- Relation to work program is well described. The gender considerations are a comparatively weak, involving gender considerations at the level of analysis only. 
- The need for early intervention is well backed by literature review provided by authors in section 1.3(a) and the rationale for prevention and early intervention is well described. 
- The proposal is innovative in that it takes the interventions into a new arena, the home environment, which is relatively neglected within mental health reserach or practice. The nature and quality of evidence on interventions in parenting also appear weaker in LMICs when compared to HICs. Rationale is strong. 
- The technology they offer to scale up is developed in limited resource settings and seems ready to scale and conduct implementation research upon. 
- The REAIM framework is well utilised for studying implementation process and effectiveness. 

**C2: Impact**
- Expected Impact well identified. 
- Strategies for enhancing impact are well outlined 
- Limited estimation of potential risks and barriers. The one risk identified of the possible multiple influences on policy influence is a relatively distant risk and a rather generic one. Other potential barriers in terms of differences varying country contexts have not been foreseen. 


**C3: Quality**
- communication pathways well identified
- Level of details in WP 2, 3 and 4 is appreciated and allows for a good determination of the feasibility and operational aspects of the project. 
- Good degree of commitment from participating institutions and commitment of resources demonstrated by these institutions. 


**Overall**
