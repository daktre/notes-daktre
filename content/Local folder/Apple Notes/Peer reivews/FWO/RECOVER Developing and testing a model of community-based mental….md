Review - these comments will be sent to the panel and to the applicant (max. 4.500 characters; ca. 750 words)

Well-written proposal. Good balance of theorising and action. Building upon existing work and collaborative relationships. Work-packages well designed and coordinated. 

The reference to Cambodia on p.5 is possibly an error(?). If not, the need to visit Cambodia may need justification in the proposal. What value does that add?

Strong justification to use a systems approach to design potential solutions for people with severe mental illnesses who are returning to community settings after treatment. Authors claim to be pioneers in setting up this kind of a community-based intervention in mental health in this country setting. They also sufficiently demonstrate technical capacity to fulfil the study objectives. 

Intervention elements reasonably well detailed. An initial theory of change/assumptions on why they expect this to work in the specific settings under considerations could have strengthened the proposal. The current care and referral cascade with intervention points in Figure 1 lacks information on the context: how the intervention elements align with the specific micro-context in which they are supposed to be implemented. 

While the bringing together of a theory of change (TOC) along with a realist evaluation is asserted as a strong point, the details of WP3 where the realist component is supposed to be developed is limited. Initially authors state that they will use a realist evaluation, but later on in p.9 while describing WP3), they call it a realist approach. In the lack of details, I wonder if they are broadly planning to inquire into “what works for whom and under what conditions” in line with the realist approach or in fact implement a full-scale realist evaluation itself. If it is the latter, then some detailing of the possible candidate programme theories and the kind of context-mechanism-outcome configurations that they will be dealing with could have been provided. In any case, WP3’s realist component needs to begin quite early even when the TOC is being developed in order to ensure sufficient time to carry out a realist evaluation. In terms of the methodology, lack of detailing out of WP3 appears to be the only limitation in my assessment.
