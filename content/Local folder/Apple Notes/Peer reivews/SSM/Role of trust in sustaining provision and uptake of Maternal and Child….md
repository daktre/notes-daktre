Abstract
Working theory and program theory distinction unclear


Introduction
- Assumption of ëxpertise to provide appropriate services to patients” may not be the only assumption in trust-related exchanges in healthcare. It could also be related to relational dynamics, familiar ties and various other drivers unrelated to assumptions of “expertise”. This is even more so in the case of people who do not themselves make the deacons on choice of hopsital/.healthcare provider such as children or mothers, the latter more so in many LMIC contexts where familial decision-making trumps individual in some circumstances if not all. The emphasis on trust exclusively related to assumptions of expertise of the provider needs to be nuanced accordingly. 
- In describing how the paper is structured, the authors attempt to provide a summary of what the existing review of trust in literature means “in the context of LMIC healthcare systems”. This is ambitious and to attempt summarising this for all LMIC healthcare systems is possibly too tall an order for only a part of this paper and perhaps needs to first explain how the authors construct “LMIC healthcare systems” itself. Reconsider this framing. 
- While explaining the Nigerian health system, authors place the TBA’s outside of the health system when they say “…and some argue that this substantial role of TBAs also indicates lack of trust in MCH services in the health system.” This is not consistent with how they have otherwise used the term health system in the rest of the paper. Explain. 
Study design
- Authors allude to a “broader mixed-methods study”. If possible provide links to the study protocol/reports through citations? Helps the reader place this study in the broader context. Similarly they later (on p.7) allude to a “main study”. This is “main” in relation to what? Clarify. 
- Authors identify that they adopted “…RE approach to examine the effectiveness of community health workers….”. While effectiveness is linked very much to the methodological purpose of RE, it is not its primary goal as is evident a few lines later. (Authors state “This enables a clear understanding of the ‘whys’ and the ‘hows’ of programme outcome within a particular context”). Explain/clarify.
- On p.8, the authors mention that they “…modelled the complex relations…”. Explain how this was done. 
- Suggestion to move the programme theory on trust into results
