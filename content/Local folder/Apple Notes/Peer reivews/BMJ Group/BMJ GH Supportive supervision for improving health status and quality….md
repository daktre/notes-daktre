Thank you for the opportunity to review this paper. The paper presents the results of a study to examine if a supportive supervision package implemented in a rural Ugandan district improves quality of care received and the overall outcomes of malnourished children. The paper is an important contribution to the limited evidence-base for interventions in limited resource settings for improving child health. 

Major comments
- Clarify inclusion/exclusion criteria. It appears that any one of the three criteria being met was sufficient for inclusion in the study. Similar for exclusion perhaps? Clarify. 
- Details of the intervention: Overall, please improve the detailing out of what constituted SS in this case. SS has been used for a wide variety of healthcare interventions and hence having a clear and concise description of what constituted SS in this case is useful. (a) “High intensity” SS: There is a need to define this clearly. Why was the SS classified as “high intensity”. What are the characteristics and how was it applied in this context. (b) Were the people implementing the intervention (such as the nutritionist for eg) recruited specially or were existing staff included into the intervention? Appears to be the latter, but good to clarify. (c) Clear details of frequency and duration: Frequency stated as bi-weekly initially. This means twice a week and once in 2 weeks, so clarify. Similarly, after the first 2 months, it was given monthly. I guess this was for 3 months? State clearly. Consider adding a schematic showing the “model”/intervention (d) Who constitutes HC staff? If they have heterogenous educational background and/or years of experience, state these either in text or in tables
- Details of the study context/setting: Please consider a paragraph or two about the study district and its broad characteristics with respect to current prevalence of malnutrition, its overall health services availability and any other health systems issues including the relative position of this district with respect to other districts in teh region/country to give the reader an idea of whether the district is among the better/worse off with respect to health services performance and/or outcomes. 
- Consider separating study limitations from the discussion (especially in the latter half)

Minor/optional comments
p.4, line 29: “related national targets”. Unclear what this means. 
p.5, line 51: “Previous recent assessments….”. Consider dropping “previous” which appears redundant. 
P.6, line 3: Expand SPHERE
