Thank you for the opportunity to review this paper. The paper is a cross-country comparative analysis of how four countries fared with respect to UHC when they merged fragmented health insurance funds/schemes/systems, a feature of health insurance & UHC architecture in various LMICs and other countries. In terms of its implications on inequity, UHC and global health, the scope of the paper is of interest to these three domains. 

**Abstract**
The results section could better capture the summary of key results obtained. At least the first two lines of the results are rather generic (even if they emerge from the authors’ analysis) and do not focus on the novel/new lessons emerging from the study in my assessment (albeit an honest summary of the results from their analysis). 

**Background** is well written and provides a useful synthesis of agenda-setting for merging health insurance funds in multiple LMIC contexts and makes a case for attempting a cross-country comparison. 

**Methods**
p.7, l 30-33: authors say that some countries were “…naturally excluded”. Given that they authors did not begin with a specific list/universe of countries (or do not declare such a list under consideration), it is unclear how such exclusions were made. Pls re-look at this section. (See for eg. they later report that “selected countries” demonstrated a diversity but the question is how such selections came about and on which countries were exclusions applied on). 

p.8, line 56: when they write “With this policy more relevant documents were extracted.” I think authors are referring to their own protocol for retaining documents for their review. Clarify. 

**Findings**
p.9, line 4: “…that acing fragmented health insurance system…”. Unclear. Clarify

Authors begin narrative description in the findings. Overall, I feel that the entire section on p.9 could do with editorial revision to sharpen their message (several use of double negative phrasing, relatively long-winded sentences; see for eg. line 53 which begins with “It is not astonishing that…..”). Also, an initial section which describes how the findings shall be presented (in relation to the methods) will be helpful. This helps better navigation in the paper. 

p.17 & 18: The phrasing “Opposition *aroused* by…”. Please review use of the word *aroused*. It normally connotes an evokation/provocation. Is that how it is meant when they say “opposition aroused by….”. 

**Conclusion**
The last paragraph is rather generic and does not specifically build upon the findings presented in the manuscript. Suggest to make the conclusions more concise and locate them within the results presented herein. 


Optional/minor comments
- Title could indicate that this is a review article
- Methods section could be sharpened to already outline how the cases are organised in terms of the commonalities of the frameworks used to describe both country case studies. 
- Manuscript needs careful review for minor grammatical errors
