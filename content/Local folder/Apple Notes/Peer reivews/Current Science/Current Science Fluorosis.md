Thank you for the opportunity to review this paper on fluorosis and iodine-deficiency disorders (IDD). The paper is an important contribution in understanding better the role of fluoride excess in our environment in shaping various health problems. The article is a “critical review” of these two conditions. I have read the paper and offer the following comments by way of peer review. I have categorised the comments into some which are major and/or essential, and others which are minor/discretionary ones for the consideration of the author. 

**Major/Essential**

**Abstract**
1. In the abstract the author appears to be alluding to independence or starting of 5-year plans? It is unclear because it has not been mentioned specifically but rather using phrases such as “…the very beginning”, 3-5 decades etc. Clarifying timelines in the abstract may be useful. 

2. In the abstract, the author mentions “the crux of the problem” as “both F‾ excess and iodine deficiency caused goiter and nine other disorders, identical in clinical manifestations”. Please clarify what is specifically meant. Is the crux of the problem the fact that they are difficult to diagnose/tell apart because of identical clinical manifestations, or rather (as indicated in the previous sentence), is it because of poor implementation (“compartmentalisation”)?

3. Author asserts that the “majority of consequences of the problem are NOT overtly visible and debilitating”. Please review. In both cases of Goitre and in the case of many kinds of fluorosis, the problems are very overtly visible no? Perhaps the author means that many people have little clinical/overt manifestations for several years before it becomes overt?

4. Author says “Guidance for scientific community to address the diseases for correct diagnosis and management in an integrated manner are dealt with” This guidance is offered to doctors (cf. correct diagnosis and management) or to the “scientific community”?

**Introduction**
1. “Malnutrition (both under and over nutrition) and non-absorption of nutrients are currently the biggest challenges facing the modern world”
Pls review. I am sure the author agrees that it is difficult to rate challenges facing the modern world and many challenges such as climate change, anti-microbial resistance etc are as big as this. Rather than get into a rating system for challenges, my suggestion is to frame this discussion within the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals. 

1. Fluoride in water is a geological problem based on drawing groundwater in specific locations within the Fluoride belt. In my understanding, Fluorosis cannot “spread” to new geographies, but rather recent extraction of water in new geographies may end up in our reports as new areas with Fluorosis. Author needs to nuance their assertions related to “spread” of Fluorosis in 2nd paragraph of Intro. 

2. Rock salt as poisonous, “common public”: Please review choice of word for something more precisely indicating what you are trying to convey. 

3. “Iodine deficiency disorders (IDD) originate from depletion of iodine in soil”: Please review sentence. Like you explain in the subsequent lines, IDD is caused by a deficiency of iodine in diet, which in turn is due to growing food in iodine-deficient soil. The geological explanation for south asian soil to have much less iodine than many other regions is depletion. This assertion needs to be modified to capture this causal cycle. 

**Critical review**
1. Methodologically speaking, critical reviews are taken up for critically evaluating a piece of text/media/book/issue. If the authors are aiming to critically review current evidence-base about Fluorosis and IDD, then I would strongly suggest to define the objectives more sharply as well as clarify the methods used to conduct the critical review clearly. Given that this is a review article, it is important to share how the current evidence was searched, how/what kind of literature was selected and how it was examined (for quality for eg) and whether for example any analytical frameworks were applied (or not). This will help better understand the choice of evidence for the review. 

2. The source of the listing of medical conditions in Table 1 needs to be included. Some of the conditions listed need to be more precise, in my opinion (brain damage for eg. whereas there are also duplications such as “mental retardation” and “intellectual disability”). This table needs to be carefully reviewed and backed up with proper citations. 

**Quality citations for major assertions**
1. In the first objective, the author states “Emergence of fluoride as a neurotoxin, hormone disruptor and an enzyme inhibitor4-7 caused not only goiter but 9 other disorders, which were identical in clinical manifestations to IDD” The references cited in favour of these do not back the assertion by the authors. Of the four references cited (which I summarise below), the first one is a recent systematic review and a meta-analysis which concludes about a “possibility of an adverse effect of high fluoride exposure on children’s neurodevelopment.” Given that this review is an overview article (rather than a meta-analysis or a systematic review), it is expected to choose authoritative references which back up assertions made (on hormone disruption for eg, and especially a good reference that supports the assertion of equivalence of medical conditions being caused by Fluorosis and IDD). 

Ref 5 is summarised above. 
Ref 5 is a study from 1988 showing “that fluoride supplementation during the final trimester of pregnancy will significantly elevate cord plasma fluoride concentrations.”
Ref 6 is a study from 1986 in an American LOW fluoride setting which has results that are contrary to the point being made. 
Ref 7 identifies five industrial chemicals as developmental neurotoxicants (does not include Fluoride): lead, methylmercury, polychlorinated biphenyls, arsenic, and toluene.

1. “Several studies have reported goiter prevalence in populations with iodine sufficiency, but further research to ascertain the cause of this has not been undertaken” 
No study cited. 

1. “Fluoride excess may be a probable cause of persistence of goiter even in iodine sufficiency.”
No study cited.

1. p.18 “In fluoride toxicity and fluorosis, it had been proven that high maternal and infant mortality was due to consumption of fluoride salts “ This needs to be carefully backed up with citations. High maternal and infant mortality has a multitude of causes including more proximate explanations. 
**Ambiguities on program related issues**
1. Author shares various program related implementation problems , but the assertions made need to be presented clearly. For eg. the entire paragraph on p.8 summarising the earlier testing with ION meter and the more recent developments are not very clearly presented. See for eg. the last sentence “These had not happened in spite of repeated requests made from various quarters.” Which are these quarters, and who has made these requests? 

2. p.9 “While the NPPCF was in operation, bottlenecks in the implementation of various components were reviewed, and suitable steps were suggested to overcome them.”
Who conducted these reviews (references?) who suggested the “suitable” steps?

1. p.11-12 the authors mention “program implementers”
This is a catch-all term for a very wide range of actors. Who is meant here? And is there any evidence to back assertions related to them becoming “ambitions”? (See “As the years passed by, the programme implementors became more ambitious as the results were highly rewarding and listed out goals to achieve besides objectives to achieve by stipulated time frame. “)



1. **Box 1**
Box 1 appears to be milestones or rather key events related to IDD in India rather than an evolution of the program itself. 

1. **Central assertion related to Fluoride excess explaining persistence of Goiter in areas with high iodised salt coverage**
This appears to be the central assertion in this critical review. However, there is insufficient evidence-based supplied by the author to consider this as a plausible explanation. I review below the challenge:
- On p. 12, where the author begins to advance this as an explanation, they mention an editorial in Indian Pediatrics journal (Kapil 2011), which is an editorial that lists 3 possible reasons for the finding of persistent goiter in areas with apparently high coverage of iodised salt: misclassification, persistence of goiter in older children even after correction of IDD (well documented elsewhere too and Kapil 2011 cites a study indeed from Ivory Coast), and concomitant persistence of Anaemia. 
The author needs to carefully address other plausible explanations before being able to advance Fluoride excess as the only explanatory factor for this phenomenon in this instance. While the evidence-base in the form of animal studies is indeed well presented, we do not yet know the degree of overlap between areas with fluorosis and the areas with persistent goiter in high iodised salt coverage areas. 

1. **Conclusion**
Authors state as one of the conclusions as “…so that correct diagnosis of the disease, and patient management provided beneficial results”. I feel the paper does not significantly address correct diagnosis and patient management. Suggest to review this conclusion carefully or else clarify what aspects of diagnosis and patient management guidelines need to be modified.

Please review sentence for clarity. What is “100% sources”?

1. p.8 mentions “highly sensitive equipment “ for F- testing. What is meant by “sensitive equipment”. Sensitive to what?
2. p.8 mentions “ION” meter in all caps. Is this capitalised for a reason? Does not appear to be an acronym
