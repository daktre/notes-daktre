I am not sure if the way of framing a bulleted list of "recommendations" is useful (or not) without there being a guiding framework or a basis for these. I offer this comment as an observation to the authors rather than a concern at this stage. I feel the preparation of recommendations (either to policymakers or for further research) should stem from a very systematic and comprehensive reivew of all evidence that exists on the topic. If the authors wish, they could include a disclaimer/limitation that mentions that the recommendations do not necessarily reflect a systematic review of all evidence. I do not deem this comment/observation as an essential requirement for publication, but rather as a suggestion to the authors and/or editor. 

I have no further comments/observations/revisions to suggest.
