The proposal makes a strong case for improving the inclusion of people with disability within health systems. It aims to do so by using an inclusion assessment tool in 3 country settings and then setting up dialogue and processes to engage health systems actors to act on the findings from the tool. The HPSR research element in this application is poorly developed although it does hold promise for possibly triggering action on disability inclusion within health systems. Although the stated aim is “to understand how to assess and strengthen…..”, the outputs foreseen are not coherent with this aim; they do not really end up providing an implementation model/framework that could aid in this, but rather seek to intervene and try to do so in their settings. There is limited clarity either in terms of a conceptual framework or in terms of a methodological approach in learning from the 3 diverse country settings in which this implementation will be done. This significantly weakens their ability to fulfil the stated aim.
