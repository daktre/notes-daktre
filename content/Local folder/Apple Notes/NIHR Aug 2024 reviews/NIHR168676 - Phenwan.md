This aims to be an intervention development & implementation research grant to co-produce an intervention to provide quality palliative care for people with Dementia in Thailand. There is a good mix of skills in the team and demonstration of policy engagement and involvement of people with diverse age, and people from under-reporesented groups in the team. 


- The need for critical discourse analysis of various international, national & local documents Is poorly justified. Unclear how/why this is being taken up and how it will inform the logic/implementaiotn model they are building
- There is lack of clarity on who will be involved in the co-production workshops. 
- The first objective is insufficient to be able to proceed to the deveopment/co-production of a logic model. The objective only seeks to explore policies & their inforluances and seeks to do this thorugh meetings and deliberative workshops. There is a risk that the outputs will not be sufficient for creation of a logic model. How will the team address structural/health systems (financing/governance/capacities/motivation - or lack thereof/Human Resources for health barriers etc) before going to co-production or logic model development. There is a risk that many of these may remain unaddressed and lead to an overall theoretical/relatively infeasible logic model which is conceptually sound but practically difficult to implement. 
- Objective 3 on testing “implementation strategies” is insufficiently developed. Will this involve choosing between multiple strategies by stakeholders? How much grounded in evidence will such choices be? What is the role of CDA in this? IN order to meet this objective of identifying evidence-based implementation strategies, the methods proposed appear to be insufficient. In the two local sites, will different strategies be used? How will they differ? Overall the reserach plan related to O3 need significant thinking thorugh in case this proceeds forward
