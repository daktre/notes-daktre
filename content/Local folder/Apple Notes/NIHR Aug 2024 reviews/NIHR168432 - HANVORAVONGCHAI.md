Strong justification for work in a remote, ethnically diverse geographical setting and which possibly needs this kind of work to be taken up. However,  a key shortcoming could be the lack of a grounded partner from the geographical location (Myanmar-Thailand border) where the applicants seek to work. While the national level institutions seem involved, there is a case to be made for greater engagement with local partners. 

Strong justification also to use the learning health systems approach to attempt problem-solving. 

How could the team overcome trust issues that have previously prevented setting up of shared/cross-border initiatives. This appears to be a big risk in WP2. Also the actual methodology/appraoch to WP2 is unclear. It is largely expressing (good) intent. For WP3 (on using vaccination as a tracer for cross-border health services coordination), again the intent is well strated, but no details provided as to *how* the applicants could achieve this. 

CEI is rather generic and does not speicifcally identify key actors who could be crucial, who could be embedded within this site and hence with experience on what kind of solutions may be locally acceptable/feasible.
