Major comments
The case for systematic reviews for health system effects (as opposed to health outcomes) is well made. However, from the desxcription it appears that the team will derive data/papers which examine health system building blocks. The SR may not sufficiently integrate complexity-aware approach to generating insights from the data/papers with this approach. 

The CEI component will need clarity on level of engagement, modalities of engagement for affected communities and how their lived experiences (if that is foreseen to be included) will contirbute to the analysis and outputs. 

This is also a topic with inherent tensions in terms of acceptance of the process/insights by policymakers and decision-makers. There does not seem to be clarity on how the research process/outputs shall engage with policymakers/decision-maekers (if at all). If not, reflexive discussion on this choice would be useful. 


Minor comments

Qual components have a relatively weaker description and it appears that the qual components are largely used in mixed methods approach with limited effort at abstraction/theorising and generating analytical generalisability for the field-building on resilience studies.
