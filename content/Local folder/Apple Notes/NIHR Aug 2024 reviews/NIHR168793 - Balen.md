Proposal builds upon ongoing work in the area of health governance and its intersections with transparency, accountability and participation in the Nepal health system. It is well conceptualised and has good likelihood of triggering dialogues around these topics at mulitple levels of the health system. The mix of skills and actors is good and there is good regional mix of settings across Nepal. The team has thought through potential impacts of various kinds ranging from academic to policy/practice levels. The biggest risk could be the difficulty in achieving design of “initiatives” that can necessarily be implemented by ‘external change agents’. It is unclear if their research is hoping to trigger internal changes/design of interventions by policy/decision-makers or if they seek to design, test/implement initiatives. The latter model is likely to be very contested given the multiple actors (including local & national governments) involved. It is unclear who will own/implement these initiatives, especially if they involve governments at any level. The scope & extent of such initiatigves too is unclear. Also unclear is the difficulty to reconcile types of interventions that are highly contextual and differ across the settings where they are being implemented.
