My role involves the running of a weekly addiction clinic within an indigenous primary care hospital setting in southern Karnataka. We have been working with the local Indigenous community for over a decade now and have recently begun co-designing a community-based program for addressing rising harmful use and dependence on alcohol and tobacco. The clinic also receives several other co-occurring mental health conditions including severe mental illnesses such as Schizophrenia and depression. The clinic also runs a training program for medical undergraduates (trainees/interns) posted from a nearby government medical college for their rural postings. Funding is through donations and philanthropies (for the hospital) and for our team from the Institute, we secure funding through research grants. Till date, the clinic has registered over 70 individuals over a 2 year time-period in the addiction/mental health clinic and with the upcoming interventions, this is likely to rise. All healthcare at the hospital we are attached to is provided free of cost to the Indigenous community patients.
