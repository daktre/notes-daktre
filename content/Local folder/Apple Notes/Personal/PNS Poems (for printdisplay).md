**To Umesh in the context of ABD Selvams botanical poems here:** <a href="https://www.researchgate.net/publication/289166540_BOTANICAL_POEMS" rel="noopener" class="external-link" target="_blank" style="color:#e4afaff;"><b>https://www.researchgate.net/publication/289166540_BOTANICAL_POEMS</b></a>

Oh Umesh, Your matte finish transcribed into lovely gloss by Suhel polish
Your sweaty skin, like your ideal teacher intended, morphed into sweet relish

Whither the Mogaral, asks Suhel,
Tui-tui, the neutered tailor bird's reflection quite male.

Finish the bloody review, you slacking ogre
Or return it to the rhyming doctor


**Response from Umesh**
O Prashanth, your rhymes make me numb
Just like our friend, Dr. A.B.D. Selvam's

"Sweaty skin/sweet relish" is beyond the pale
As is your rhyming Suhel with male

Rhyming doctor, you are abysmal
Your poetry is naught but dismal
