To 

Executive Director
The National Health Systems Resource Center
Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
Government of India


Sir,

The Sangha is a collective of the Soliga forest dwelling tribal communities in Chamarajanagar district and has been working for the welfare and development of tribal people in this district over two decades now. Our focus in the last five years has been mainly land and forest rights, health, and tribal development, biodiversity conservation and education of our people in the district. In the last five years, we have begun partnering with local NGOs like VGKK, ATREE, and research organisations like IPH Bangalore for participatory research and action on health of the tribal people in our district. We also support and partner with state-level collectives of tribal peoples in Karnataka.

We are glad to support the proposed innovation and learning Centre for comprehensive primary healthcare of tribal communities being steered by the national health systems resource Centre. On behalf of our Sangha I would like to convey our support for such a centre. In case this is successful we hope to partner with VGKK in participatory design and implementation of programs for improving health and wellness of tribal communities in the district.
