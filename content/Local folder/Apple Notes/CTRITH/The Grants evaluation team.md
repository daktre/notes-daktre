Madam/sir,

Sub: Regarding request for minor revisions to Principal Investigator (PI) & Co-PI time commitments and corresponding budgetary changes in our CRC grant

We have recently been awarded a grant titled **Centre for Training, Research and Innovation in Tribal Health (CTRITH)** and the grant is expected to begin on **October 1, 2021**. Due to the 6 month delay in initiating the grant from the planned start date in May 2021 as well as due to ongoing adjustments we are making in response to the 2nd wave of COVID-19 pandemic in Chamarajanagar district (where most work of the Centre will be based out of), we have had to make a minor change in the time commitments foreseen by myself (lead PI) and my co-PI, Dr. Upendra Bhojani (also from Institute of Public Health Bengaluru). 

The change we propose is as follows:
1. Dr. Prashanth’s time (lead PI) commitment is needed from second half of Y1 (instead of Y2 i.e. 6M earlier) at the same percentage of time committed for the remaining years (40%).
2. Dr. Upendra Bhojani (co-PI)’s time is similarly needed from Y3 onwards (instead of mid-Y3 i.e. 6M earlier) with an increase in time commitment from 20% to 40% - a reassessment based on the changed situation based on need for more fieldwork/on-field availability. 

This imposes a shortfall of Rs. 16,20,000. We have been informed that we may not have access to additional funds to meet this shortfall. 

We have identified the following budget lines under “Miscellaneous” head of Transferable funds which are not mission critical that cover this shortfall. These are ‘e-learning development and course fees & certification’ and ‘mentoring fees for institutions’. We are willing to submit an undertaking stating that these activities will be completed as planned and we will raise resources for other sources as needed.

Given this commitment and the critical need for the 2 PIs time be focused on implementing the CTRITH grant, **we request for an exception and an approval to shift the funds** across these heads without affecting the grant amount approved in anyway. 


We look forward to your favourable response. 
 Yours truly


Dr. Prashanth N Srinivas
Faculty & Lead PI
