To 

Executive Director
The National Health Systems Resource Center
Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
Government of India


Sir,

I have been informed by Dr. Tanya Seshadri about the proposed setting up of Innovation and Learning Center expression of interest by the National Health Systems Resource Center. As the District RCH Officer, we have partnered with VGKK in improving healthcare of tribal communities, particularly on maternal and child health, malnutrition as well as on screening of Sickle Cell Disease. In case the ILC comes to our district, it will be of great benefit to many of our PHCs that serve tribal populations. In line with our commitment to improve healthcare among tribal communities, we support the setting up of the ILC in Chamarajanagar and are happy to provide support in case it comes through.
