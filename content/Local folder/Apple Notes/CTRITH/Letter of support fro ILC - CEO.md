To 

Executive Director
The National Health Systems Resource Center
Ministry of Health & Family Welfare
Government of India

I am informed by Dr. Tanya Seshadri of VGKK about the NHSRC’s expression of interest in setting up an Innovation and Learning Center for comprehensive primary health care in indigenous/tribal areas. I am writing this letter in support of this Intiative. 

VGKK is uniquely placed in being able to convene communities (including tribal community-based organisations), researchers and impelementers/decision-makers at district, state and local levels in accomplishing this task. Their four decades of stellar work in promoting an integrated approach to tribal development with health at the center is relevant for being able to set up such a center. Their strength has been their openness to engage in meaningful and empowering relationships with tribal community based organisation of the Soliga people. Indeed, the Soliga community’s ability to obtain various rights including forest rights through community action has been due to the community organisation work that VGKK and other NGOs carried out in the 1990s and 2000s. More recently during the 2nd wave of COVID-19, VGKK has stepped up and partnered with Institute of Public Health, Bengaluru & other NGOs in the district and with the Zilla Panchayat in strengthening the Gram Panchayat level response through capacity-building and designing  solutions adapted to local context. For instance, their role in partnering with other NGOs and with us in setting up our district’s first Adivasi COVID care center is worth mentioning. 

Overall, the location of the ILC in our district will also be beneficial to all other Adivasi communities in the district. And we hope that the learnings from such a center shall also be relevant to other tribal areas in the state and country. In case this ILC moves forward, the Zilla Panchayat and health department look forward to working for strengthening health and wellness of tribal communities through this. All the outputs of this initiative shall be shared with the district health team and there shall be no financial implications of this initiative on the Zilla Panchayat. 

Yours truly
