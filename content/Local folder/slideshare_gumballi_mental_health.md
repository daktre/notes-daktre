# Presentation: Mental health in primary health care: The Gumballi experience

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/mental-health-in-primary-health-care-in-india-the-gumballi-experience/234504210)

## Context
A review of the "Gumballi experience"—a decentralized, community-based approach to integrating mental health into primary care in a rural district.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways
*   **Integration Gap**: Despite policy intent, mental health often remains siloed from primary care.
*   **Community Success**: The Gumballi model showed that with typical primary care resources (and some training), mental health access can be significantly improved.
*   **Traditional Healers**: Mentions partnerships with traditional healers as a pragmatic strategy to reach patients.
