# Presentation: Variable regulation in Indian states and labour migrations

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/social-security-variable-regulation/16920009)

## Context
Research on how diferences in state-level regulations affect **labour migration** and social security for migrants.

## Core Arguments
*   **Fragmentation**: Social security benefits are often tied to domicile, leaving migrants vulnerable when they cross state lines.
*   **Portability**: Need for portable social security benefits.
