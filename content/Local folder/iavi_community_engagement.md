# Video Resource: Community Engagement during COVID-19

**Event**: IAVI Webinar Series - "COVID-19: Ask the Experts"
**Date**: May 20, 2020 (approx.)
**Platform**: IAVI YouTube Channel / Webinar
**Role**: Expert Speaker

## Context
Hosted by the International AIDS Vaccine Initiative (IAVI), this webinar focused on a critical dilemma during the pandemic: **How to maintain community engagement when social distancing makes physical interaction impossible?** It addressed the "social" aspect of the virus, particularly for vulnerable populations.

## Core Arguments & Key Takeaways

### 1. The Paradox of "Social Distancing"
Dr. Srinivas and panelists discussed the terminology and impact of "social distancing."
*   **Argument**: While *physical* distancing was necessary, *social* connection was more vital than ever. The term itself could be harmful if it implied social abandonment of vulnerable groups.
*   **Takeaway**: Public health messaging needs to be careful with language to avoid stigmatization and isolation.

### 2. Building Trust Remotely
*   **Argument**: Trust is hard to build without face-to-face interaction, yet it is essential for policy implementation.
*   **Takeaway**: Organizations had to pivot to creative, remote methods to keep lines of communication open. Existing relationships (social capital) built *before* the pandemic became the lifeline regarding who the community listened to.

### 3. Policy Design vs. Reality
*   **Argument**: Policies designed in boardrooms often fail on the ground if they don't account for the reality of how communities live (e.g., shared toilets, crowded housing).
*   **Takeaway**: "Community Engagement" isn't just about telling people what to do; it's about feedback loops where community reality informs policy modification.
