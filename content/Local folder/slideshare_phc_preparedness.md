# Presentation: Corona in India: PHC Preparedness and lockdown effects

**Type**: SlideShare Presentation
**Link**: [View Presentation](https://www.slideshare.net/slideshow/corona-in-india-phc-preparedness-and-lockdown-effects/234127311)

## Context
An analysis of how Primary Health Centers (PHCs) in India coped (or failed to cope) with the sudden lockdown and pandemic demands.

## Core Arguments
*   **Disruption**: Routine services collapsed as PHCs shifted entirely to COVID work.
*   **Preparedness Gap**: PHCs lacked basic PPE and protocols in the early days.
