Being a growing field that has sought to challenge the ways and means of knowledge production in public health and indeed the nature of knowledge itself that is deemed valuable in global health (see for instance Seye Abimbola's [The uses of knowledge in global health](https://gh.bmj.com/content/6/4/e005802)), the idea of journals as the final home for HPSR knowledge has been a contested topic. And to muddle those waters further the issue of predatory journals makes it even problematic. There is a need to imagine better the kind of platforms needed for HPSR-ish knowledge for which a note is at [[Journals for health policy & systems research]]
