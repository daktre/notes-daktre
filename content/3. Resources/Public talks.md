I tend to invest in agreeing for teaching or speaking engagements in anticipation of a good piece of writing. Because the teacing and speaking are good "tests" for the analyis and content...of course audience depending. :) 

This page is a running index of talks, teaching lectures, panels, podcast episodes and a stubborn parallel strand of my IRL intrests in bird quizzes and natural-history stuff.

The list is reverse-chronological. **Theme** is a tag, not a section, because a talk on tribal health is usually also a talk on health systems, and forcing it into one bucket loses that. Use the theme column, or the site search, to cut across the timeline.

**Links** are only shown where they exist: `slides` goes to SlideShare, `video` to YouTube or the host's channel, `event` to a conference or host page. A dash means the asset exists somewhere but is not yet uploaded, and is on my list to fix.

## Themes

- **Equity**: health equity, structural determinants, and who gets left out of averages
- **Adivasi health**: tribal health, forest rights, cultural safety, the THETA and rTribe arc
- **Health systems**: primary health care, UHC, urban and district health governance
- **Methods**: implementation research, realist evaluation, philosophy of science, research practice and careers
- **One Health**: zoonoses, biodiversity, planetary health
- **Birding**: bird quizzes, natural history, citizen science
