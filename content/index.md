This is a life-project with an after-life leap of faith.  It aims to be a publicly chronicled meaning-making in progress across diverse thematic areas. Some of these co-occur merely because they are of interest to me. Over time through prolific note-taking, reading, curation, sense-making through dialogue and conversation, and sometimes through maturation of these insights like good wine, and at other times for lack of a good fermentation process as vinegar, both find a place here at Crossroads | ಅಡ್ಡರಸ್ತೆ. 

It's a kind of an effort to move beyond blogs and websites towards an inter-connected personal knowledge-base but with public archival value.
