## Review
Lovely book. Deeply developed characters with intricate details of cancer treatment from multiple patient perspectives from the days of radiotherapy in 60s, with plenty of details on life in post-Stalin Russia. My first Solzhenitsyn. Long read and many characters not specifically “developed” for the sake of completeness but left to the readers imagination. Lovely discussions between doctors and between the doctor and the protagonist on health system design, healthcare experience itself and the philosophy what is called patient-centredness in cancer care...
