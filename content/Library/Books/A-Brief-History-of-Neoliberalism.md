## Review
Rather more academic than I imagined, but a good overview of neoliberal thinking and its progress. China as a case has been discussed in much greater detail than Sweden and Korea. Much recommended for anyone studying any aspect of social policy and/or development. I was looking for a good overview of the historical events that have shaped neoliberal thinking and I think the book fulfilled this.
