## Review
Given my close following of events surrounding trump election and my recent interest in law, I thought the report would be an engaging read. It was. By definition, it will be interesting for those with more than a little familiarity with events preceding and subsequent to Trumps election victory. Quite a bit of drama especially in the synthesis of narratives of trump associates.
