## Review
Lovely conversations with RBG . Each conversation is themed around a chapter and with concise and informative context by the author. Many of the conversations give a peek into how RBG turned out the way she did with her personal reflections on what shaped her opinions. Expectedly conversations are heavily on gender equality and civil liberties. A must read for anyone interested in law and politics in general.
