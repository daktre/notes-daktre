## Review
Continues the trend of a very detailed and lovely storyline interwoven yet pursuing several parallel threads. Having already finished 7 seasons of the TV series, the departures from the TV script are quite apparent, although not (yet?) very major. The role of Beric Dondarion is much stronger in the book, and one of the most chilling epilogues about Lady Catelyn (not part of the TV series). All in all, a lovely read and yet sustaining my interest enough to pick up Book #4!
