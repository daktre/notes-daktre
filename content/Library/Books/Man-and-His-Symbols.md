## Review
Lovely read. Perhaps the time and space one is at matters In order to read and/or “feel” the book. Some chapters (the one by Jung and Von Franz) are less abstract than the ones by Jaffe for instance. Had to let go of some broad brushes on “Hindu”, “African”, “primitive” stereotypes rooted in what is essentially a elegant yet Eurocentric view. Overall an engaging and immersive experience.
