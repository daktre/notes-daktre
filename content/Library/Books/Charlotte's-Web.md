## Review
Ideal night time reading for children. As engaging for adults as it is for children. Got this as a gift from a visiting colleague. A gentle introduction for children to joy, disappointment, loss/bereavement, longing and of course lots of fun in barn life - all through the eyes of Wilbur the pig.
