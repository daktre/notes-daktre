## Review
One of the few places where irreverent and insolent are complimentary. George Carlin is like the conscience of the often heightened and uncritical bordering on ridiculous linguistic sophistication of our times rendered through his unfiltered and unfettered expletive punctuated speedy delivery that’s rare among many comedians I’ve seen.
