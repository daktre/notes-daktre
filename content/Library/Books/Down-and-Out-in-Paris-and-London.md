## Review
Lovely first-person account of days spent in the streets of Paris and London by the author. A very different type of travelogue (in his own words) that provides a first person account of what poverty could  be like. Wonder if theres a contemporary Indian first-person account of life in any of our cities; could be darker and drearier, given that we still do not have the broken city Socia welfare system that the author seeks a reform of....
