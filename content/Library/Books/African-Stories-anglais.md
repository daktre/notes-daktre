## Review
A mixed bag for me. Some stories were deeply moving and insightful and showed me the amazing kinship of post-colonial writing, others were steeped heavily in a context that I understood little about as if I needed the storyteller to join me over a fireside for a chat on some aspects. But overall super refreshing the writing style, themes and the closeness to rural Indian settings
