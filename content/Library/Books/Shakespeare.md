## Review
A comprehensive but rambling account of Shakespeare from as many sources as Bryson could find. Along with brief history of nearly everything, this is one other book of bryson that is unlike all of his travelogue books. Not very funny, quite dry at certain moments, but appears quite scholarly. Perhaps a better bet for somebody very driven to know about Shakespeare rather than a general reader.
