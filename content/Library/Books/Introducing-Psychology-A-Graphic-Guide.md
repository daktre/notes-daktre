## Review
Book is quite basic and can hardly be described as a graphic novel. Rather a compilation of definitions with semi-illustrative cartoons, typically one every page. While the language of the definitions is simple and clear, I was hoping for a clearer discussion and deeper introduction to psychology history and concepts.
