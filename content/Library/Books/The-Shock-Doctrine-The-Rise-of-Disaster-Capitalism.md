## Review
A powerful narrative on how US-style neo-liberal thinking is much more than a mere political ideology. The author indicates (through thorough analysis of documents and events in history) that an agenda to dismantle 'states' to safeguard "potential harm" to future US interests was pursued by several US administration. A decent understanding of economics and world affairs beforehand helps.
